Combining a kitchen with a room - what to check before the work

Combining a kitchen with a room depends on three things: whether the wall between the rooms is load-bearing, whether the kitchen has a gas hob and how the space will be ventilated.

A non-load-bearing wall can be demolished with a construction notice; in a load-bearing wall only an opening is possible, with an explanatory memorandum. Wall demolition costs from €6/m², documents by estimate.

The wall between the kitchen and the room

According to the Riga municipality's explanation, for demolition of a non-load-bearing wall to join rooms, an architect prepares a plan, and a construction notice is submitted in BIS as simplified renovation. If the wall is load-bearing, it is not demolished completely: an opening in it is possible as simplified reconstruction, with an explanatory memorandum, a structural engineer's solution and the consent of more than half of the apartment owners.

In published plans, kitchens in the 602 series are mentioned at about 7–8 m², in the 119 series 6–7 m², in the 104 series 8–9 m². But for the 602 series it is also published that the internal walls are usually load-bearing, so in these buildings the wall must be checked first. Which walls are load-bearing can be told from the building's design documents and a structural engineer's assessment.

The Riga municipality's reply on LV portāls of 04.12.2025; Cabinet Regulation of 02.09.2014 No. 529, paragraphs 7.¹ and 7.². Series data: varianti.lv and nopo.lv. Checked 11.11.2026.

A gas hob in a combined room

If the kitchen has a gas hob, combining the rooms involves the gas rules. A user, in agreement with the gas supplier, may replace or move within one room a gas hob and other appliances whose combustion products are not discharged into a flue. An appliance with a flue, for example a gas boiler, needs the supplier's permission even in the same room, a report on the condition of the flue and ventilation ducts, and a technical solution. Moving to another room or installing new equipment needs the supplier's technical conditions, a report on the flue and ventilation ducts, and a building design. The technical solution for moving gas pipes is prepared or approved by a person with the relevant certificate.

Gas work is not part of the renovation estimate. If you will combine a kitchen that has a gas hob, first contact the gas supplier, and in your request write that the kitchen has gas.

Cabinet Regulation of 30.06.2015 No. 336, LBN 241-15 (internal natural gas pipelines), paragraphs 7 and 9 and sub-paragraph 2.15. Checked 11.11.2026.

Ventilation and extractor hood

Kitchens in residential buildings are ventilated through natural exhaust ducts. If a room has a gas hob and no constant air exchange, it is prohibited to connect a mechanical ventilation device to the natural ventilation duct, and an extractor hood with a fan is usually regarded as such a device. If there is a common ventilation system, the kitchen air duct may be connected only to its own duct.

In a combined room, cooking smells also spread into the living area, so the type of hood and where it is connected must be decided before the wall is demolished.

LBN 231-15 (heating and ventilation), sub-paragraph 85.4 and paragraph 90.²; Cabinet Regulation of 19.04.2016 No. 238, sub-paragraph 89.10 (the Fire Safety Regulations currently in force). Checked 11.11.2026.

The order of work when combining a kitchen with a room

  1. Wall, gas, ventilation Find out the type of wall, the gas situation and the hood solution.
  2. Documents A plan and a notice or explanatory memorandum in BIS, consents where they are needed.
  3. Demolition The wall or opening is formed according to the approved plan, with the wiring disconnected beforehand.
  4. Floor In the kitchen and the room the floor level and covering can differ: they are joined with levelling or screed.
  5. Electrics The kitchen area usually needs more sockets; when the wiring is reconstructed, it is fitted with residual current protection.
  6. Finishing Walls, ceiling and floor covering in one room, then the kitchen units.

Frequently asked questions

Can a kitchen be combined with a room if there is a gas hob?

Replacing or moving a gas hob within one room is agreed with the gas supplier; an appliance with a flue needs the supplier's permission even in the same room, and a report on the condition of the flue and ventilation ducts, while moving to another room needs technical conditions, a report on flues and ventilation, and a building design. First contact the gas supplier.

Can a kitchen and a room be joined in a 602 series apartment?

For the 602 series it is published that the internal walls are usually load-bearing. Complete demolition is then not the solution, but an opening can be assessed by a structural engineer, with an explanatory memorandum and the association's consent. More on the page 602 series apartment renovation.

What kind of extractor hood should go in a combined kitchen?

If there is a gas hob, a hood with a fan must not be connected to the natural ventilation duct where the room has no constant air exchange. Check this before ordering the furniture.
