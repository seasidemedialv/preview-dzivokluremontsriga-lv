How a three-room apartment renovation differs from a smaller apartment

Renovating a three-room apartment is not just a larger area. There are more rooms, and the decisions are linked: the wiring runs through the whole apartment, the floor levels between rooms have to match, and a bathroom is hard to renovate while someone lives in the apartment. So the list of work here is longer, and the order matters more.

The layout depends on the building series. The published layouts of three-room apartments in the 602 series mention a bathroom combined with a toilet and an extra separate toilet by the entrance and a kitchen of about 8 m². For a three-room apartment in the 119 series the layouts mention a separate bathroom with two toilets and a 7 m² kitchen, but the layouts in this series differ. For a three-room apartment in the 104 series, one bathroom and toilet unit and an 8 m² kitchen are mentioned. Check your apartment by the plan or measurements, not by the name of the series.

In “Khrushchyovkas” the area of a three-room apartment is given as about 51 m², and the rooms are often walk-through. This changes the amount of work and whether you want to change the layout. Series pages: 602 series apartment renovation, 119 series apartment renovation, Khrushchyovka renovation.

Whole-apartment renovation or room by room

If the apartment is empty, the renovation can be done in the whole apartment in one sequence: demolition everywhere, then utilities, floors and walls, and the finish at the end. This way no job has to be repeated, for example wires are not pulled through an already painted wall.

If you continue to live in the apartment, the renovation can be divided into stages: first one or two rooms, then the kitchen and bathroom, the hallway last. What to keep in mind in that case is written on the page renovation while living in the apartment.

It is better to plan the electrical wiring for the whole apartment at once, even if the rooms are renovated one at a time. From 01.07.2025, when the electrical installation in an existing building is rebuilt, it must be equipped with residual current protection. The Fire Safety Regulations in force provide for an inspection of the electrical installation every 10 years.

Amendments to LBN 261-15 (in force from 01.07.2025) on residual current protection; Cabinet Regulation No. 238 of 19.04.2016, paragraphs 56 and 57, on the inspection of the electrical installation. Checked 11.11.2026.

Work order in a three-room apartment

  1. List of work What to change in each room: walls, floors, doors, wires, pipes.
  2. Demolition The old finish, floor covering, tiles and, if needed, partitions are removed in all the rooms being renovated.
  3. Utilities New electrical wiring, pipes within the apartment, radiators. A riser is common property, so replacing it needs the consent of the apartment owners' association, and the building manager organises the work.
  4. Floors and walls Screed or floor levelling, wall plastering and skimming. At this stage the drying time of materials matters.
  5. Bathroom and kitchen Waterproofing, tiling, installing plumbing, finishing the kitchen walls and floor.
  6. Finishing Painting or wallpaper, floor covering, interior doors, skirting boards and window sills.

What to decide before renovating a three-room apartment

  • The type of renovation in each room: full everywhere or, for example, full in the bathroom and kitchen but cosmetic in the rooms.
  • Whether you will change the layout. Removing a wall or a new opening may need documents, see apartment replanning.
  • Whether you will change riser sections. Risers are the common property of the apartment owners, so work on them needs the association's consent, and the building manager organises the work.
  • Whether you will choose the materials yourself. Write this in your request, because the materials part of the estimate depends on it.
  • When the apartment will be free and whether anyone will live in it during the renovation.

In your request, it helps to give the address, the apartment area in m², the building series or year of construction, which rooms to renovate and the condition of each room. If you have the apartment plan, you can send it to the specialist when they contact you.

How much does a 3-room apartment renovation cost?

Cosmetic renovation: from €100/m² of apartment area. Full renovation: from €280/m² of apartment area. Turnkey renovation: from €600/m². Prices are for labour. Materials are calculated separately in the estimate. Construction waste removal is not part of the renovation estimate.

What this work includes

  • Demolition and preparation of all rooms, if full renovation is chosen
  • Wall, ceiling and floor work according to the list of work
  • Installing floor covering, doors and skirting boards
  • In a full renovation, new electrical wiring and pipes within the apartment

What is calculated separately

  • Materials: tiles, paint, floor covering, doors, plumbing
  • Riser work (common property: the association's consent is needed)
  • Replanning documents, if walls are changed

Frequently asked questions

How much does a full renovation of a three-room apartment cost?

A full renovation costs from €280/m² of apartment area, materials separate. For a 51 m² apartment that is from €14,280 for labour. The final amount depends on the condition of the walls and floors and the chosen finish.

Can only one or two rooms be renovated?

Yes. In your request, write which rooms to renovate and what kind of renovation each needs. Prices for separate jobs, for example painting or laying laminate, are on the page apartment renovation prices.

Does a 4-room apartment renovation have a different price per m²?

The published “from” prices per m² do not differ. In a larger apartment the area and the list of work change, and so does the total.
