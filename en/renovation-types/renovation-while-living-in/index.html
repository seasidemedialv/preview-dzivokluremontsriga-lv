Renovation while living in the apartment - in stages

Renovating while living in the apartment is possible if it is divided into stages: one room or one group of work after another, while the other rooms stay available for living.

The hardest part is the only bathroom and the kitchen, because they cannot be used for a while. Cosmetic renovation costs from €100/m², and the price can be calculated for each stage separately.

What changes if people live in the apartment during the renovation

In an empty apartment the work is done over the whole area in one sequence. If people live in the apartment, you have to choose which room is free for the renovation, where to put the furniture and how to use the bathroom and kitchen on the days when work is going on there. So a renovation in stages can take longer.

Not every renovation suits living alongside. A cosmetic renovation, room by room, can usually be done. A full renovation with demolition, new wiring and screed in all rooms, however, means that for a time the apartment has no electricity in some rooms and no usable floor. In that case it is worth considering moving out for the renovation or dividing the stage by parts of the apartment.

How to divide a renovation into stages

  1. Work that affects the whole apartment It is better to plan the wiring and pipes for the whole apartment in the first stage, so that already renovated walls do not have to be damaged later.
  2. The first room Renovate the room in which the furniture from the next one can later be placed. It becomes the place for living and storage.
  3. The other rooms One at a time, moving the furniture into the room that has already been renovated.
  4. The kitchen Plan where to cook on the days when the kitchen has no water or electricity.
  5. Bathroom and toilet If there is only one bathroom, this is the stage to plan in the most detail, because the materials must be allowed to dry before use.
  6. The hallway Usually the last, because materials and rubbish are carried through it throughout the renovation.

The bathroom and material drying

How long a renovation takes depends on the amount of work and the drying time of the materials. In the bathroom this is set by the material manufacturers. For Ceresit CL 51 waterproofing, the manufacturer states that the second coat can be applied after about an hour and tiling can start about 2 hours after the second coat. With Ceresit CM 17 tile adhesive, grouting on a floor can be done after at least 24 hours. The manufacturer of Ceresit CE 40 grout allows water loading after 24 hours, but it reaches full strength after 5 days.

These times apply at normal temperature and humidity, and they add up. So in an apartment with only one bathroom it is worth deciding in advance where to wash during this time. If the toilet is in a separate room, it can be renovated in a different stage from the bathroom.

Technical data sheets of Ceresit CL 51 (11/2025), CM 17 (10/2026) and CE 40 (05/2026). The times depend on temperature, base and air humidity. Checked 11.11.2026.

Dust, furniture and paint

Furniture that cannot be taken out is best stacked in the middle of the room or in another room and covered. In your request, write which rooms will stay occupied and where the furniture will be, and it can then be taken into account in the list of work.

Do not press furniture against a freshly painted wall straight away. For Tikkurila Euro Smart 2 paint, the manufacturer states the second coat after 2 hours, and the paint reaches its final hardness in about a month. For the Ceresit CN 76 floor leveller, the manufacturer allows walking on it after 3 hours, but tiling after 3 days.

Noise and neighbours

If you live in the apartment, the noise affects you too. It is better to tell the neighbours about the renovation in advance. Disturbing a person's peace can be punished with a fine of up to 100 fine units, but the Riga Municipal Police explains that everyday domestic noise is usually not an offence. The apartment owners' association can set house rules, so ask the building manager whether your building has set renovation hours.

The Administrative Penalties Law (public order), Section 11(1); explanation of the Riga Municipal Police in LV portāls, 12.06.2026; Law on Residential Properties (Dzīvokļa īpašuma likums), Section 10. Checked 11.11.2026.

Frequently asked questions

Can you live in the apartment during a full renovation?

Partly, if the renovation is divided by parts of the apartment. If demolition, rewiring and screed take place in all rooms at the same time, living in the apartment is difficult, and it is worth considering moving out for that period.

How long will the bathroom be unusable?

How long a renovation takes depends on the amount of work and the drying time of the materials. For example, the manufacturer of Ceresit CE 40 grout allows water loading after 24 hours. An exact list of work can be drawn up after seeing the apartment.

Does a renovation in stages cost more than everything at once?

The prices per m² are the same. The total can be affected by the fact that work covering several rooms, such as wiring, has to be planned at once, otherwise the finished surfaces have to be opened up later.

Can you be fined for renovation noise?

Disturbing a person's peace can be punished, but everyday domestic noise is usually not an offence. Ask the building manager whether the building has set renovation hours. More on the page when renovation work is allowed.
