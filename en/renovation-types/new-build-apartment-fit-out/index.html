New-build apartment fit-out - from grey or white finish to move-in ready

New-build apartment fit-out is the work that makes an apartment in a grey or white finish ready to live in: tiles in wet rooms, wall finishing, floor coverings, doors and plumbing.

Grey finish and white finish: what to check

See the purchase contract or the developer's finish description for what exactly the grey or white finish includes in your apartment. The names are the same, but the content can differ between developers, so the list of work depends on what has been done in the apartment. More on the differences in the article grey or white finish.

In your request, information about the following will help:

  • whether the floor has a screed and how level it is
  • whether the walls are plastered or already skimmed
  • where the electrical outlets and the distribution board are
  • where the water and drainage outlets are in the bathroom and kitchen
  • whether the heating is by radiators or underfloor heating
  • where the ventilation ducts are in the kitchen, bathroom and toilet

In your request, write what finish is already there; you can send the apartment plan and photos to the specialist when they contact you.

New screed and moisture

In a new building the screed and plaster may still be damp. Manufacturers give figures for this. The technical data sheet of Ceresit CM 17 tile adhesive says that a cement screed and plaster under tiles must be at least 28 days old with moisture below 4%.

Under laminate, parquet and flexible coverings the requirements are stricter. The Ceresit CN 76 technical data sheet gives the moisture of a cement base as up to 2.0% without heating and up to 1.8% with floor heating. So before the floor covering, the moisture of the base must meet the limits stated by the manufacturer.

Ceresit CM 17 and CN 76 technical data sheets. Checked 11.11.2026.

Order of work in a new apartment

  1. Description and condition Compare the finish description with the actual condition of the apartment.
  2. Electrical and lighting plan Before the wall finishing, it is decided where extra sockets and lighting points are needed.
  3. Wet rooms In the bathroom and shower, waterproofing is applied before tiling. The manufacturer of Ceresit CL 51 recommends at least two coats, and waterproofing tape is worked into corners and joints.
  4. Walls and ceilings Skimming, priming and painting.
  5. Floor coverings When the moisture of the base meets the manufacturer's limits, laminate, vinyl flooring or parquet is laid.
  6. Completion Interior doors, skirting boards, plumbing, light fittings.

What to decide before the work

If you want to change the layout, non-load-bearing partition walls belong to the apartment. The Riga municipality explains that for removing a non-load-bearing wall a certified architect prepares a plan and a construction notice is submitted in BIS. If approval is needed, write this in your request.

When planning a kitchen extractor hood, take into account LBN 231-15: the ventilation ducts of kitchens and sanitary rooms may be connected to a common ventilation system only each in its own duct. And one more thing: in your request, write whether you will choose the materials yourself.

Law on Residential Properties (Dzīvokļa īpašuma likums), Section 3; Riga municipality, LV portāls 04.12.2025; LBN 231-15, sub-paragraph 85.4. Checked 11.11.2026.

Frequently asked questions

In a new development, is the screed already ready for tiles?

Not always. The Ceresit CM 17 technical data sheet requires a cement screed under tiles to be at least 28 days old with moisture below 4%. In a fresh building the screed may not yet be dry enough.

In a white finish, do the walls still need skimming?

It depends on the condition the walls are handed over in. If the surface is ready for painting, priming and painting are enough; if there are irregularities, skimming comes first.

Can the layout be changed in a new apartment?

Non-load-bearing partition walls belong to the apartment, but for removing them in Riga a construction notice is submitted. More in apartment replanning.

Can you fit out the whole apartment in one order?

Yes, then it is turnkey apartment renovation from €600/m². If only a few jobs are needed, they are charged line by line.
