Renovation estimate - what it contains and how to read it

A renovation estimate is a list of work in which each item has a quantity (m² or pieces), a unit price and a sum, while materials are usually listed separately.

What a renovation estimate consists of

An estimate is made of three things: what has to be done, how much, and at what price. The quantity multiplied by the unit price gives the sum of each line, and the sum of the lines is the total for the work. Materials, for example tiles, paint, floor covering and plumbing fixtures, are in a separate part or a separate estimate.

  • Work: for example “wall skimming”, “laminate installation”, “toilet installation”.
  • Unit: m² of apartment area (for the renovation of the whole apartment), m² of wall or floor surface (for individual work) or pieces (doors, points, radiators).
  • Quantity: how many m² or pieces. For wall work this is the area of the walls, not the floor area.
  • Unit price and the sum of the line.
  • What is not included: materials, removal of construction waste, documents, other work.

How to check an estimate before signing

  1. Compare the quantities There are more m² of walls than m² of floor. If an estimate gives the floor area for wall work or the other way round, ask why.
  2. Check the units A price per m² of apartment area and a price per m² of surface cannot be compared. Compare offers only in the same units.
  3. Find what is not included Materials, removal of construction waste, documents and work on common property, for example risers, may be outside the estimate.
  4. Agree in writing A contract concluded orally can be valid, but in a dispute the customer has to prove what was agreed and what was paid. That is why it is better to put the estimate and the contract in writing.

On the oral contract and evidence: explanation of the Latvian Council of Sworn Advocates on LV portāls 26.11.2025. Checked 11.11.2026.

What to send to receive a renovation estimate

In the request the following will help: the address, the apartment area in m², the building series or year of construction, which rooms to renovate, what work is needed and the condition of each room. If you will choose the materials yourself, write that too.

An exact estimate can be made after seeing the apartment. Some things cannot be seen from a description or a photo: the evenness of the walls, the condition of the floor under the old covering, the condition of the wires and pipes.

The prices used to calculate an estimate

For the renovation of the whole apartment: cosmetic renovation from €100/m², full renovation from €280/m² of apartment area, turnkey renovation from €600/m². Prices for individual work are on the page apartment renovation prices. Prices are for labour. Materials are calculated separately in the estimate. Construction waste removal is not part of the renovation estimate.

The estimate total is affected by the apartment area in m² and the number of rooms, cosmetic or full renovation, the condition of the walls, floors and ceilings, whether the electrical wiring, pipes and risers are changed, and the chosen finish. Frequently asked questions

Can a renovation estimate change after the work has started?

If damage is discovered under the finish, for example a damaged floor base, the list of work may change. We agree the price and the list of work before the job starts.

Why does a bathroom estimate have so many lines?

A small room has many separate jobs: demolition, waterproofing, tiling, installation of the drain, toilet and bath. Each of them is its own line with its own unit.

Does an estimate have to include materials too?

Materials can be included in a separate part, or the estimate can list only their quantity if you buy them yourself. Our published prices are for labour.

How do I compare two renovation estimates?

Compare each job with the same unit and quantity, and check what is not included in each estimate. Two totals without a list of work cannot be compared.
