Full apartment renovation

A full apartment renovation means the apartment is renewed down to the structure: the old finishing is removed, new wiring and pipes are installed, the floors and walls are levelled and only then is the finishing put in.

In Riga and the Riga region it costs from €280/m² of apartment floor area. It is chosen when the wiring and pipes are worn out and new finishing would not last long on them.

When a full renovation is needed

A cosmetic renovation is enough if everything under the finishing is in order. A full renovation is needed when the problem is in the walls and floor: old wiring, pipes with leaks, cracks in the plaster or a floor that is not level. An explanation on LV portāls says that in many homes the wiring was installed in the Soviet era and is 30–50 years old.

The word “eiroremonts” (Euro-style renovation) is also used with the same meaning: the whole apartment renewed. We call it a full renovation, and the price is on one line.

The Fire Safety Regulations currently in force require an inspection of the electrical installation once every 10 years, with an inspection report. If you have such a report, state in the request when it was drawn up: it shows the condition of the wiring.

Cabinet Regulation No. 238, paragraphs 56 and 57; LV portāls on electrical installation inspection. Checked 11.11.2026.

What changes and who owns it

The apartment owns the non-load-bearing partitions, all the finishing, the doors and the utilities up to the common-property risers. The owner may renew them without the agreement of other owners if the work does not affect common property or other apartments.

Risers, load-bearing walls and floor slabs are common property. Radiators are also common property if their operation depends on the building's heating. If a full renovation requires replacing a riser section or a radiator, it affects common property, so the consent of the apartment owners' association is needed, and the building manager organises the work.

When the electrical installation of an existing building is rebuilt, since 01.07.2025 it must be fitted with residual current protection. An apartment in an apartment building must also have a stand-alone smoke detector, at least one on each floor of the apartment, and a full renovation is a good moment to decide its place on the ceiling.

Law on Residential Properties, Sections 3, 4 and 11; LBN 261-15 amendments of 17.06.2025; Cabinet Regulation No. 238, paragraphs 119 and 120. Checked 11.11.2026.

The order of work in a full renovation

  1. Job list A job list with quantities is the basis of the estimate.
  2. Demolition The old finishing, floor coverings, tiles and, if needed, non-load-bearing partitions are removed. For construction waste removal, see buvgruzuizvesanariga.lv.
  3. Wiring and pipes New cables are pulled in, places for sockets and switches are made, and the water and sewage pipes are replaced up to the risers.
  4. Plastering and screed The walls and floors are levelled. In the Ceresit CM 17 technical sheet the manufacturer states that a cement screed and plaster under tiles must be at least 28 days old.
  5. Wet rooms In the bathroom and kitchen waterproofing is applied and tiles are laid.
  6. Walls and ceilings Skimming, priming and painting, or wallpapering.
  7. Floors, doors, plumbing The floor covering is laid, and the interior doors, skirting boards, plumbing, sockets and switches are installed.

What the customer decides before the work

  • where the sockets, switches and light points will be in each room
  • whether you will change the layout; for demolishing a non-load-bearing wall, according to Riga municipality's explanation, a construction notice is submitted, more in apartment replanning
  • whether risers and radiators have to be replaced; they need the association's consent, and the building manager organises the work
  • whether you will choose the materials yourself
  • whether the building has its own renovation hours; ask the building manager

Write these answers in the request. We agree the price and the list of work before the job starts.

Full renovation: costs

Full renovation: from €280/m² of apartment floor area. Prices are for labour. Materials are calculated separately in the estimate.

Examples: a 1-room apartment of 33 m²: 33 × €280 = from €9,240 for labour. A 2-room apartment of 50 m²: 50 × €280 = from €14,000 for labour.

Common-property parts have separate lines: replacing a riser section in an apartment from €100 per riser, radiator replacement from €115 per radiator. The price is affected by:

  • the apartment's floor area in m² and the number of rooms
  • cosmetic or full renovation
  • the condition of the walls, floors and ceilings
  • whether the wiring, pipes and risers are replaced
  • the chosen finish (tiles, floor covering, doors)

Frequently asked questions

Does a full renovation need documents from the building board?

Finishing work needs no documents. Before the work, find out from the building board (in Riga, the City Development Department) whether replacing the utilities needs a notice. If you demolish a non-load-bearing wall, a construction notice is needed, and an opening in a load-bearing wall needs an explanatory memorandum (Cabinet Regulation No. 529). If an approval is needed, write that in the request.

Can you stay in the apartment during a full renovation?

It is hard, because for some time there is no water or electricity and the floor screed is drying. If you still have to live there, see renovating while living in the apartment.

How long does a full renovation take?

How long a renovation takes depends on the scope of work and the drying time of the materials. For screed and plaster under tiles manufacturers state a minimum age, for example at least 28 days in the Ceresit CM 17 technical sheet.

Does the riser have to be replaced too?

The riser is common property, so replacing it needs the consent of the apartment owners' association, and the building manager organises the work. If it is worn out, it is better to do it during a full renovation while the walls are open.
