Budget apartment renovation - what to change and what to keep

What a budget renovation is and what it usually includes

A budget renovation is not a separate type of work, but a choice of what to do and what not to do. Usually it is a cosmetic renovation: walls and ceilings, floor covering, doors and skirting boards. The existing wiring and pipes stay, so there is no need to cut the walls and pour new screed.

Preparation (skimming from €10/m²) can cost more than painting (from €4/m²). If the wall is even, painting is enough. If it has cracks and holes, skimming is needed. So the budget is affected most by the condition of the walls and floors, not only by the choice of materials.

Cheap renovation: where you can save

  • Painting instead of wallpaper, or the other way round. Painting walls: from €4/m² of wall surface, hanging wallpaper: from €5/m². Both need a prepared wall.
  • Laminate or vinyl flooring on a level base. Laminate installation: from €6.50/m², vinyl flooring installation: from €10/m². If the old floor is level, levelling is not needed.
  • Only the rooms where it is really needed. You can renovate one room and the hallway, leaving the rest for the next stage.
  • Doors only where the old ones no longer close or are damaged. Interior door installation: from €100 per door, doors separate.
  • You choose the materials yourself. In your request, write whether you will choose the materials yourself.

Where we do not recommend saving in a budget renovation

In a bathroom, waterproofing is work you can no longer see after tiling, but it protects both your apartment and the neighbour's below. The manufacturer of Ceresit CL 51 waterproofing intends it for walls and floors before tiling in wet rooms and recommends at least two coats. Waterproofing in the bathroom: from €6/m².

Electrical wiring must be checked every 10 years, and the results are recorded in a report. In many homes the wiring was installed in the Soviet era and is 30–50 years old. If the wiring in the apartment is old, it is worth checking it before a cosmetic renovation, because after new paint or wallpaper, replacing wiring means damaging the walls again.

Ceresit CL 51 technical data sheet (11/2025); Cabinet Regulation No. 238 of 19.04.2016 (“Ugunsdrošības noteikumi”, Fire Safety Regulations), paragraphs 56 and 57 (the rules currently in force); LV portāls on the age of electrical wiring. Checked 11.11.2026.

The order of work in a budget renovation

  1. List of work For each room, write what stays and what changes. The more precise the list, the more precise the estimate.
  2. Preparation Old wallpaper or flaking paint is removed, cracks and holes are filled and, where needed, the walls are skimmed.
  3. Ceilings and walls Ceilings first, then walls. Absorbent and skimmed surfaces are primed before painting.
  4. Floor The floor covering is laid when the wall work is finished, so that it is not damaged.
  5. Doors and skirting boards At the end, doors, skirting boards and other details are fitted.

Frequently asked questions

Is a budget renovation the same as a cosmetic renovation?

Usually yes: the finish is changed, but the wiring and pipes stay. The difference is that in a budget renovation you choose only the work and rooms that are necessary. More on the page cosmetic renovation in an apartment.

Is it cheaper to paint or to hang wallpaper?

The labour price is similar: painting from €4/m², hanging wallpaper from €5/m². In both cases the wall must be prepared, and preparation can cost more than the covering itself.

Can I buy the materials myself?

Yes. In your request, write whether you will choose the materials yourself.

Does a budget renovation need any permit?

If only interior work is done in the apartment, for example painting walls, hanging wallpaper or changing the floor covering, no construction documents are needed. If walls or openings are changed, check the page does a renovation need a permit.
