What we do

  • Apartment renovation types: cosmetic, full and turnkey renovation, new-build fit-out.
  • Room renovation in an apartment: bathroom, kitchen, rooms, hallway, balcony.
  • Renovation work in an apartment: walls, tiles, floors, ceilings, doors, wiring, risers, radiators.
  • Apartment replanning: demolishing walls, openings, combining rooms.
  • Private house interiors and renovation and renovation of business premises.

What can be determined before an inspection and what cannot

From the area, a plan, the building series and a description, a starting price can be named using the published lines. From a description or photos you cannot see what the plaster under the wallpaper is like, what wiring and pipes are in the walls and how uneven the floor is under the old covering. That is why an exact estimate can be made after seeing the apartment.

We agree the price and the list of work before the job starts. How long a renovation takes depends on the scope of the work and the drying time of the materials.

How the price is set

Prices are for labour. Materials are calculated separately in the estimate. The prices are published on the page apartment renovation prices as “from” prices per m² of apartment area, per m² of surface or per piece. They depend on the area, the type of renovation, the condition of the walls and floors, the replacement of services and the chosen finish.

Why only a form

The price of a renovation is determined by details: the address, area, building series, work and condition of the rooms. If they are in the request, the specialist can name a more exact price. After you submit the form, we contact you within 5 minutes. The form is on the page contact.

What we do not do

  • Gas work is not part of the renovation estimate: moving gas appliances needs the gas supplier's approval and a technical solution.
  • We do not remove construction waste: see buvgruzuizvesanariga.lv.
  • We do not do separate plumbing repairs, for example replacing a tap: see santehnikisriga.lv.
  • We do not treat rooms against mould: see dezinfekcijariga.lv.
  • We do not install air conditioners.

Languages

The site is in Latvian, English and Russian. You can write your request in any of these languages.

Frequently asked questions

Does a request commit me to anything?

No. A request is a request to be contacted and given a price. You decide about the work after the price and the list of work are known.

Can I choose the materials myself?

Yes. In the request, say whether you will choose the materials yourself. In the estimate they are separate from the labour.
