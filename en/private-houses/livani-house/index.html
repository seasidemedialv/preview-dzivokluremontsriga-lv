What to find out before the estimate

Each house may have been altered differently, so write what you know about your house. An exact estimate can be made after seeing the house.

In the request write what you know about the house: the year of construction, whether the walls are boarded, whether the floors are wooden, whether there has been earlier renovation. If you have a plan of the house or a technical inventory file or cadastral survey file, you can send it to the specialist when they contact you. The more is known before the estimate, the fewer surprises after demolition.

What to check in an older house before demolition

  • Asbestos: asbestos cement was used for slate roofing, pipes and boards, and old vinyl floor tiles and their adhesive may contain asbestos. It cannot be reliably identified by appearance, only in a laboratory. Damaged asbestos is dangerous because the fine fibres get into the airways.
  • Electrical wiring: in many homes the wiring dates from the Soviet era and is worn out. When rebuilding the installation in an existing building, residual current protection is needed; for socket and lighting circuits no more than 30 mA.
  • Gas: a gas hob can be moved within one room in agreement with the gas supplier; moving it to another room needs technical conditions and a building design.
  • Floors: under laminate, Quick-Step requires levelling unevenness greater than 2 mm per 1 m.

For more on asbestos, see the article asbestos in an apartment during renovation.

Sources: European Commission SAMANCTA; RSU seminar materials; LV portāls 2023 and 02.07.2025; LBN 261-15, paragraph 28.1; LBN 241-15, paragraphs 7 and 9; Quick-Step instructions LV. Checked 11.11.2026.

Documents

Interior finishing work needs no documents. If load-bearing walls, floors between storeys or the volume of the house are changed, it is a reconstruction or renovation that needs an explanatory memorandum or a building design, and work starts only after the building board's note. Ask the building board of your municipality.

Sources: Construction Law (Būvniecības likums), Section 1, paragraphs 4 and 9, and Section 17; Cabinet Regulation No. 529 (“Ēku būvnoteikumi”, Building Construction Regulations), paragraphs 1.¹, 7.1 and 7.³. Checked 11.11.2026.

Order of work

  1. Initial data In the request state the structure of the walls and floors, the utilities and any traces of damp.
  2. Demolition The old finish; suspicious materials are sent for analysis before being damaged.
  3. Utilities Electrical wiring under the direction of a certified construction specialist, pipes.
  4. Walls and floors Plasterboard lining or plastering, floor levelling.
  5. Finishing Skimming, painting, tiles, floor covering.

How long the renovation takes depends on the amount of work and the drying time of the materials.

Frequently asked questions

Is there definitely asbestos in a Līvāni house?

No, that cannot be said without analysis. Asbestos cement was common in slate, pipes and boards, and old vinyl floor tiles may contain asbestos, but it is determined only in a laboratory.

Does interior work in a Līvāni house need a project?

Not for finishing work. If you change load-bearing walls, floors between storeys or the volume of the house, documents are needed under the Building Construction Regulations.

Can only one floor be renovated?

Yes. Write which rooms and how many m² you will renovate, and whether you will live in the rest of the house during the work.
