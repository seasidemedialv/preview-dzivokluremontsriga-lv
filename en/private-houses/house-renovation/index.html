How a house renovation differs from an apartment

In a house everything is yours: the load-bearing walls, the floor slabs, the stairs, the services from the inlet to the last socket. There is no building manager responsible for risers, and no owners' association whose consent you must ask. This gives freedom, but it also means that the owner decides on the condition of the structures and on the documents.

In an old house, layers of finish can hide traces of earlier renovations: several floor layers, old wires under the plaster, pipes in the walls. That is why the estimate can be refined once it is visible what is under the finish.

When a renovation needs documents

  • For interior work that does not involve renovation, reconstruction, restoration or demolition, no construction intent documents are needed.
  • Simplified renovation means improvements to a building without changing the load-bearing structures, the volume and the use, and without affecting the facade.
  • Reconstruction changes the volume of the building or strengthens load-bearing elements; renovation is the replacement of worn load-bearing elements without changing the volume and load-bearing capacity.
  • In cases not named in the regulations, a building design is prepared and a building permit obtained. With an explanatory memorandum or a building permit, work may begin only after the building board's mark.

For work without a required explanatory memorandum or building permit, a private individual can be given a warning or a fine of up to 400 fine units (up to €2,000). Ask the municipal building board which document your work needs; more in the article does a renovation need a permit.

Sources: the Construction Law (Būvniecības likums), Section 1, points 4 and 9, Section 17; Cabinet Regulation No. 529 (“Ēku būvnoteikumi”, Building Construction Regulations), paragraphs 1.¹, 7.1 and 7.³. Checked 11.11.2026. The Construction Law, Section 25; the Administrative Liability Law (Administratīvās atbildības likums), Section 16.

Asbestos and old wiring

Before demolition in buildings built before the asbestos ban, the employer finds out whether they may contain materials with asbestos. Asbestos has historically been used in the thermal insulation of pipework and boilers, and asbestos cement was used for slate, pipes and boards. Asbestos cannot be reliably identified by appearance; it is determined in a laboratory.

In many homes the wiring was installed in the Soviet era, and the wires are worn out. New wiring is installed under the supervision of a certified construction specialist, and when it is reconstructed in an existing building, residual current protection is needed.

Sources: Cabinet Regulation No. 852, paragraph 25; European Commission SAMANCTA; RSU seminar materials; LBN 261-15; LV portāls 2023 and 02.07.2025. Checked 11.11.2026.

The renovation work in sequence

  1. List of work What stays and what has to be changed: the condition of the structures, floors and services.
  2. Demolition Removing the old finish, floor layers and partitions.
  3. Services Electrical wiring, water supply and sewer inside the house.
  4. Floors and walls Screed or floor levelling, plastering, plasterboard partitions and ceilings.
  5. Wet rooms Waterproofing, tiling, installing sanitaryware.
  6. Finishing Skimming, painting, floor covering, doors.

How long a renovation takes depends on the scope of the work and the drying time of the materials. The manufacturer Ceresit states that a cement screed and plaster under tiles must be at least 28 days old.

Source: Ceresit CM 17 technical data sheet 10/2026. Checked 11.11.2026.

What the interior renovation work includes

What this work includes

  • demolition back to the structure;
  • screed, floor levelling, plastering;
  • plasterboard partitions and ceilings;
  • waterproofing and tiling;
  • skimming, painting, floor covering, installing doors.

What is calculated separately

  • materials, sanitaryware and the doors themselves;
  • replacing wiring and pipes (in an apartment electrical wiring is from €20/m², in a house by estimate);
  • documents, an architect, a structural engineer;
  • roof, facade, foundations and the heating boiler.

Frequently asked questions

Does a house renovation need a building permit?

Not always. For interior work no documents are needed. If load-bearing structures or the volume are changed, an explanatory memorandum or a building design is needed, and work starts after the building board's mark.

Can a house be renovated floor by floor?

Yes. In that case, write in your request which floor you want renovated first and whether you will live in the house during the work, because the order of work and the connection of services depend on it.

What should be done if possible asbestos is found during demolition?

Do not damage the material and have it sent for laboratory analysis. Work with asbestos is regulated by Cabinet Regulation No. 852 and supervised by the State Labour Inspectorate.
