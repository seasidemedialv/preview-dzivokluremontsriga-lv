Attic renovation

Attic renovation is interior work in rooms under the roof, where the walls and ceilings are often sloping and each room has its own shape.

An attic room in a house and an attic apartment

In a private house, the owner decides about the roof and its insulation, observing the building regulations. In an apartment building the roof is common property, so if the roof leaks in an attic apartment or there are traces of damp by the roof, you must first turn to the building manager, and only then plan the finishing.

Before finishing, check that there are no traces of damp or mould under the roof. If there are, the cause must be dealt with before a new finish, otherwise it will be spoiled.

Source: Law on Residential Properties (Dzīvokļa īpašuma likums), Section 4(1) point 1. Checked 11.11.2026.

Loft renovation: when documents are needed

If a loft is being converted into a living space, find out what document is needed before the work. Simplified renovation under the Building Construction Regulations (Ēku būvnoteikumi) means improvements that do not change the load-bearing structures, the volume and the type of use and do not affect the façade. If roof window openings have to be made in load-bearing structures or the type of use of the building changes, a different document is needed.

Ask the municipal building board, and if approval is needed, write that in your request. For interior work that does not involve renovation or reconstruction, no documents are needed.

Sources: Cabinet Regulation No. 529, paragraph 1.¹ sub-paragraphs 5 and 6, sub-paragraph 7.1. Checked 11.11.2026.

Plasterboard in sloping ceilings and partition walls

The frame of a Knauf dry-construction partition is formed of CW profiles (studs) and UW profiles, which are fixed to the floor and ceiling. In sloping ceilings the frame is fixed to the roof structure, and then plasterboard sheets are put on. Between them there is usually thermal insulation; its type and thickness are chosen according to the roof structure.

An attic has many corners and sloping planes, so the amount of skimming and painting is greater than in a room with straight walls. The work is calculated per m² of lined surface.

Source: Knauf LV product page on the CW profile. Checked 11.11.2026.

Order of attic renovation

  1. Inspection The roof structure, traces of damp, height, floor base.
  2. Demolition Removing the old lining and floor, if needed.
  3. Electrics Cable routes and light fitting positions before the lining; installed under the direction of a certified construction specialist.
  4. Frame and lining Profiles, thermal insulation, plasterboard ceilings and partition walls.
  5. Floor Levelling the base and the covering.
  6. Finishing Skimming, painting, doors.

How long a renovation takes depends on the scope of work and the drying time of the materials.

What to write in your request

  • the address and whether it is a private house or an apartment in an apartment building;
  • the attic floor area in m² and the approximate height at the highest point;
  • whether it is a lived-in attic or the loft will only be converted into a room;
  • whether you will choose the materials yourself.

Frequently asked questions

Can a loft be converted into a room without documents?

No. Converting a loft into a living space needs construction documents; in an apartment building the loft is common property, so the association's consent is needed too. Ask the building board.

Who pays for a roof leak in an attic apartment?

In an apartment building the roof is common property, so you have to talk to the building manager about it. Restoring the finish of the apartment can be planned once the cause has been dealt with.

Can underfloor heating be laid in an attic?

Yes. Underfloor heating installation starts from €16/m²; the electrical wiring for it is installed under the direction of a certified construction specialist. See underfloor heating installation.
