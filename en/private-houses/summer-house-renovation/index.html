In a summer house the most important question is temperature, because finishing materials have their own requirements. Interior work in summer houses is offered in Riga and the Riga region; painting starts from €4/m², laying laminate from €6.50/m².

Why temperature matters in a summer house

Material manufacturers state working conditions, and in an unheated house these cannot always be met. So before the work, think about whether the house can be heated during and after the work.

  • Paint: the manufacturer of Tikkurila Euro Smart 2 requires a dry surface with a temperature of at least +5 °C and air humidity up to 80%; the second coat can be painted after about 2 hours at +20 °C.
  • Waterproofing: Ceresit CL 51 is applied at +5 to +30 °C.
  • Laminate: Quick-Step recommends keeping the panels in unopened packaging for 48 hours at room temperature in the room where they will be laid.
  • Self-levelling compound: the manufacturer of Ceresit CN 76 states the drying times at +23 °C and 50% air humidity; in a cooler room they are longer.

Sources: Tikkurila Euro Smart 2 data sheet; Ceresit CL 51 and CN 76 data sheets; Quick-Step laminate installation instructions LV. Checked 11.11.2026.

Summer house work

  • Renewing walls and ceilings: skimming and painting, or plasterboard cladding.
  • Floors: levelling the base and laminate or vinyl flooring. Quick-Step requires unevenness greater than 2 mm per 1 m to be levelled out.
  • A shower or small bathroom: waterproofing, tiling, installing a floor drain.
  • Partitions: a separate bedroom with a plasterboard partition.

If the summer house has rooms under the roof, see the page attic renovation for them. If the house is wooden and the floor creaks or sags, the base must be checked first. If the summer house is in a building divided into apartment properties, the common-property rules are the same as in an apartment.

Order of work

  1. Condition of the rooms The condition of the floors and walls, and whether the house can be heated (according to your description in the request).
  2. Preparation Removing the old finish, priming the surfaces.
  3. Floors and walls Levelling, plasterboard, skimming.
  4. Wet rooms Waterproofing in two coats, tiles, installing the floor drain.
  5. Finishing Painting, floor covering, skirting boards.

How long the work takes depends on the amount of work and the drying time of the materials.

What to write in your request

  • the address and how the house can be reached by vehicle;
  • which rooms and how many m²;
  • whether the house has heating and electricity during the work;
  • the condition of the walls, floors and wet rooms;
  • whether you will choose the materials yourself.

Frequently asked questions

Can a summer house be renovated in winter?

It can, if the rooms are heated during the work to the temperature that the material manufacturers require, for example at least +5 °C for paint and waterproofing. In your request, write whether the house has heating.

Can laminate be laid in an unheated summer house?

This is determined by the instructions of the particular laminate manufacturer. Quick-Step, for example, recommends keeping the panels at room temperature for 48 hours before laying. When choosing a covering, read the manufacturer's requirements for unheated rooms.

Can a shower with a floor drain be built in a summer house?

It can, if there is a water supply and sewerage. Installing a floor drain starts from €100 per drain, and the slope and tiling are added; see building a shower with a floor drain.
