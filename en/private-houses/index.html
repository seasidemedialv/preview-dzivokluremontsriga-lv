Which page to choose

These pages are about the inside of the house. The roof, facade and foundations are other work, and we do not describe them here. If you need only one room, for example a bathroom or kitchen, see the room pages too; they apply to both an apartment and a house.

Private house renovation is the largest job: in an old house the finish is removed down to the structure, the wiring and pipes are changed and the floors, walls and bathroom are built anew. It also covers when a renovation needs documents.

Summer house renovation is about a seasonal house that is not heated in winter. Finishing materials have temperature and humidity requirements, so it matters when and how the house can be heated during the work.

Attic renovation covers rooms under the roof: plasterboard walls and sloping ceilings, partitions, floors and finishing, as well as converting a loft into a living space.

Līvāni house renovation is interior work in an older private house, where the structures and materials can differ from house to house, so they have to be found out first.

Sauna finishing is the interior work for a sauna, washroom and rest room in a house or a separate building; in the washroom, waterproofing is decisive.

What is different in a house from an apartment

A detached house usually has no apartment owners' association and building manager, so the approvals needed in an apartment because of common property are usually not relevant. The building regulations, however, apply to all buildings.

  • For interior work that does not involve renovation, reconstruction or demolition, construction documents are not needed.
  • If the work changes the volume of the building or reinforces load-bearing elements, it is a reconstruction; replacing worn load-bearing elements is a renovation. This work needs documents, and the work starts only after the building board's mark.
  • Wiring is installed under the supervision of a certified construction specialist; when it is rebuilt in an existing building, residual current protection is needed.
  • Within one room, a gas hob and other appliances without a flue may be replaced or moved in agreement with the gas supplier. An appliance with a flue needs the supplier's permission and a certificate on the condition of the flue and ventilation ducts even in the same room. Moving to another room needs the gas supplier's technical conditions and a building design; the solution for moving gas pipes is prepared or approved by a certified person. Gas work is not part of the renovation estimate.

Which document is needed for your particular house depends on the building and the work; ask the building board of your municipality. If approval is needed, write that in the request.

Sources: Construction Law, Section 1 paragraphs 4 and 9, and Section 17; Cabinet Regulation No. 529 (“Ēku būvnoteikumi”, Building Construction Regulations), paragraphs 1.¹, 7.1 and 7.³; building code LBN 261-15 (internal electrical installations); LV portāls, 02.07.2025; building code LBN 241-15 (internal natural gas pipelines), paragraph 9. What to write in the request

  • the address and the year the house was built, if you know it;
  • what the house is built from: wood, masonry, blocks or something else;
  • which rooms and how many m² you will renovate;
  • whether the house is heated in winter;
  • whether you will choose the materials yourself.

What interior work includes

What this work includes

  • demolition of the old finish;
  • plastering, skimming and painting walls;
  • floor levelling and laying the floor covering;
  • plasterboard partition walls and ceilings;
  • tiling and waterproofing in wet rooms.

What is calculated separately

  • materials, plumbing, doors and stoves;
  • wiring and electrical points;
  • documents and approval from the building board;
  • roof, facade and foundation work.

Frequently asked questions

Do private house interiors need a permit?

For interior work that does not involve renovation, reconstruction or demolition, documents are not needed. If load-bearing structures are affected or the volume is changed, documents are needed under the Building Construction Regulations; ask the building board of your municipality.

Do you work outside Riga too?

Yes, in Riga and the Riga region, for example in Ķekava, Jūrmala and Sigulda. Write the address in the request.

Do house prices differ from apartment prices?

Prices are for labour, materials separately in the estimate, and they are calculated per m² or per unit. In a house the list of work can include more floors, stairs and sloping ceilings. An exact estimate can be made after seeing the house.

When will you contact me after a house request?

After you submit the form, we contact you within 5 minutes. If you state the area and rooms in the form, the list of work can be discussed straight away in the conversation.

Can I order just one room, for example a bathroom?

Yes. Bathroom renovation (all work) starts from €3,000; more detail on the page bathroom renovation.
