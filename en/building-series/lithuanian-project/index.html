What the Lithuanian project is

In Riga, 464 series buildings are called the “Lithuanian project”. Wikipedia writes that the series was built from the early 1960s, and an improved version from 1966. The Ministry of Economics study assessed 13 buildings of this series commissioned in 1964–1978.

The buildings are 5 storeys, made of grey concrete panels with gypsum-concrete partition walls. In Riga they are in Iļģuciems, Purvciems, Āgenskalns, Imanta, Dzirciems, Bolderāja, Ziepniekkalns, Ķengarags, Jugla, Mežciems and elsewhere.

Listings use the terms “old” and “new Lithuanian project”. For a renovation, more important than the name is the plan of the particular apartment: the ceiling height and the size of the bathroom and toilet, and of the kitchen, depend on the particular apartment.

Sources: Wikipedia “Tipveida dzīvojamā apbūve Rīgā” (standard residential development in Riga), Ministry of Economics study in LV portāls 2020, cityreal.lv. Checked 11.11.2026.

What may be worn in these buildings

The housing site nopo.lv mentions as typical 464 series problems waterproofing, worn risers and electrical wiring, poor ventilation on the lower floors, and the fact that panel walls are hard to replan. The Ministry of Economics study of 2020 found the inspected buildings safe for continued use; for buildings of the later type, the roof U-shaped panel may have insufficient bearing depth.

  • A riser is common property, so replacing it needs a decision of the apartment owners' association, and the work is organised by the building manager. Access is left at the risers, for example a hatch, because the owner must allow the building manager's specialists to inspect the building's services.
  • Old wiring: in many homes the electrical wiring was installed in Soviet times, and the Fire Safety Regulations currently in force require it to be tested every 10 years.
  • Ventilation: kitchens, toilets and bathrooms in residential buildings are ventilated through natural extraction ducts. If a room has a gas cooker, a mechanical ventilation unit must not be connected to the natural ventilation duct unless there is constant air exchange.

Sources: nopo.lv on the 464 series; Law on Residential Properties (Dzīvokļa īpašuma likums) (Sections 4 and 10); Cabinet Regulation No. 238 (“Ugunsdrošības noteikumi”, Fire Safety Regulations) (paragraphs 56, 89.10); LBN 231-15 (heating and ventilation) (paragraph 90.²); LV portāls 2023. Checked 11.11.2026.

Lithuanian project 3-room layout and replanning

If in a three-room apartment you want to combine the kitchen with a room or move a door, gypsum-concrete partitions and panel walls must be told apart: load-bearing walls are common property, and an opening in them needs an explanatory memorandum, an architect, a structural engineer and, if the building is divided into apartment properties, the consent of more than half of the apartment owners.

Which wall is load-bearing is determined from the building documents or by a structural engineer's survey. In your request, write what you want to change; you can send a plan or photos to the specialist when they contact you. On the kitchen, read the page combining a kitchen with a room.

What to write in the request

  • the address and the building series or year of construction;
  • the apartment area in m² and the number of rooms;
  • what rooms the apartment has and what condition they are in;
  • which floor the apartment is on (on the ground floor, pay attention to ventilation and moisture);
  • whether you will change the wiring, pipes and riser sections;
  • whether you will choose the materials yourself.

Frequently asked questions

Are Lithuanian project buildings safe to live in?

The Ministry of Economics study, which LV portāls wrote about in 2020, found the 13 inspected 464 series buildings safe for continued use. For buildings of the later type, the study pointed to the bearing of the roof U-shaped panel. Ask the building manager about the condition of your own building.

Can a kitchen be combined with a room in a Lithuanian project?

If there is a non-load-bearing partition between them, according to the Riga municipality's explanation an architect's plan and a construction notice in BIS are needed. If the wall is load-bearing, an opening is a simplified reconstruction that needs an explanatory memorandum and, if the building is divided into apartment properties, the consent of more than half of the apartment owners. A gas cooker may be replaced or moved within one room in agreement with the gas supplier. Moving it to another room needs the supplier's technical conditions, a report on the flue and ventilation ducts, and a building design. The technical solution for moving gas pipelines is prepared or accepted by a certified person.

What should I know about ventilation on the ground floor?

The housing site nopo.lv mentions poor ventilation on the lower floors as a 464 series problem. During the renovation, keep the natural extraction ducts in the kitchen and bathroom working, and write in your request where you see signs of moisture.
