104 series apartment renovation

Renovating a 104 series apartment in Riga has to be planned around the reinforced concrete internal load-bearing walls: they are common property, and an opening in them is made only with an explanatory memorandum and a structural engineer's solution.

In a one-room apartment the bathroom and toilet are usually combined, in a two-room apartment they are separate, and the kitchen is 8–9 m². We do cosmetic and full renovation of apartments in the 104 series in Riga and the Riga region.

What is typical of a 104 series building

The 104 series is a prefabricated panel building from the 1970s with 9, 12 or 16 storeys. The external walls are aerated concrete panels, and the internal load-bearing walls are reinforced concrete panels. These buildings have no rubbish chute. In Riga they are in many neighbourhoods: Purvciems, Pļavnieki, Ziepniekkalns, Zolitūde, Imanta, Mežciems, Dzirciems, Iļģuciems, Čiekurkalns, Mežaparks and Jugla.

The kitchen is about 8 m² in a one-room apartment, 9 m² in a two-room apartment and 8 m² in a three-room apartment. A three-room apartment has one bathroom and toilet unit. The ceiling height depends on the particular apartment, so measure it before choosing a ceiling.

Load-bearing walls: what to know before an opening

For an opening in a load-bearing wall, according to the Riga municipality's explanation you need an architect and a structural engineer, who inspect the structures and calculate the solution for the opening. An explanatory memorandum is submitted to the building board as a simplified reconstruction, with the structural solution for the opening and an assessment of its effect on the strength of the building.

The work may start only after the building board has recorded in BIS that the conditions for starting construction work have been met. The consent of the apartment owners' association is also needed: the owners who represent more than half of the apartment properties must vote for it. For construction work without an explanatory memorandum, a private individual can be fined up to 400 fine units (up to €2,000).

Cabinet Regulation No. 529, sub-paragraph 7.² 8, sub-paragraphs 24.4.2 and 24.4.3; Construction Law (Būvniecības likums), Section 17(1) and Section 25(1); Law on Residential Properties, Section 16(3). Checked 11.11.2026.

A combined bathroom in a one-room apartment

In a combined bathroom, a bath or shower, a toilet, a washbasin and also a washing machine have to fit into a small area. The main choice is a bath or a shower with a floor drain: a shower needs a floor slope towards the drain and careful waterproofing. The manufacturer Ceresit recommends applying CL 51 waterproofing in at least two coats and setting waterproofing tape into the joints between wall and floor.

In residential buildings the bathroom, toilet and shower are ventilated through natural extract ducts, so do not cover the ventilation grilles. For more on the work: combined bathroom renovation and building a shower with a floor drain.

Radiators and risers in a tall building

A radiator in an apartment is common property if its operation depends on the building's heating system. So replacing a radiator affects common property and needs the consent of the apartment owners' association; shutting down and refilling the heating system must be agreed with the building manager. The same applies to risers: they serve all floors, and in an apartment only a riser section can be replaced; this needs the association's consent, and the building manager organises the work.

In a building with 12 or 16 storeys, write the floor and whether there is a lift in your request. For radiators: radiator replacement in an apartment.

Law on Residential Properties, Section 4(1) paragraph 1 and Section 11(3). Checked 11.11.2026.

How a renovation usually goes

  1. Approvals For radiator replacement the association's consent is obtained, for replacing a riser section the association's consent is obtained; for an opening in a load-bearing wall the documents are prepared.
  2. Demolition The old finish, the bathroom tiles, the floor covering and, if needed, the old pipes are removed.
  3. Utilities New pipes, electrical wiring and radiators.
  4. Bathroom Floor slope, waterproofing, tiling, installing plumbing.
  5. Rooms and kitchen Wall plastering and skimming, painting, floor covering, doors.

How long the renovation takes depends on the amount of work and the drying time of the materials.

How much a 104 series apartment renovation costs

Cosmetic renovation: from €100/m² of apartment floor area. Full renovation: from €280/m² of apartment floor area. Bathroom renovation (all the work): from €3,000. Radiator replacement: from €115 per radiator. Prices are for labour. Materials are calculated separately in the estimate. Construction waste removal is not part of the renovation estimate.

Example: for a 33 m² one-room apartment, full renovation is 33 × €280 = from €9,240 (labour). The price is affected by the area and the number of rooms, the condition of the walls and floors, whether the wires, pipes and risers are changed, and the chosen finish. Frequently asked questions

Can radiators in a 104 series apartment be replaced as you choose?

Radiators that depend on the building's heating are common property, so replacing them needs the association's consent, and shutting down the system must be agreed with the building manager. If the building's heating system changes, the design is agreed with the building manager.

Can a shower be built instead of a bath in a combined bathroom?

Yes. A shower with a floor drain needs a floor slope and waterproofing across the whole wet area. Installing a floor drain: from €100 per drain, slope and tiling separate.

Are 104 series buildings safe?

In a study by the Ministry of Economics the series had no series-wide defects; individual shortcomings were found in the joints, balcony fixings and the roof. These are common-property matters, handled by the association and the building manager.

Can a door opening in a load-bearing wall be widened in a 104 series building?

Only with a structural engineer's solution, an explanatory memorandum, the building board's record in BIS that the conditions for starting are met and the association's consent. More: creating a door opening.
