Cookies

This page describes which cookies the website dzivokluremontsriga.lv uses and how you can manage them. The website does not use advertising cookies and does not build visitor profiles for advertising.

Prepared by Dzīvokļu Remonts Rīga, updated 12 November 2026. Sources: 1

What cookies are

A cookie is a small text file that a website saves in your browser. It lets the website remember, for example, your choice about cookies. Some cookies disappear when you close the browser, others are kept for a set time. The browser's local storage works in a similar way, and the same rules apply to it.

Which cookies we use

At present dzivokluremontsriga.lv does not use cookies: there are no statistics, advertising or social network tools, and the request form works without cookies. That is why no cookie notice is shown on the website.

If we add visit statistics in the future, they will work only with your consent, a cookie notice with the option to change your choice will appear on the website, and this page will be updated.

Storing information on a user's device is allowed with the user's consent; consent is not needed if it is necessary to provide a service requested by the user. Source: Information Society Services Law (Informācijas sabiedrības pakalpojumu likums), Section 7.¹ (likumi.lv). Checked 03.11.2026.

How to manage cookies in your browser

In all common browsers (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge), the privacy settings let you:

  • view the stored cookies;
  • delete all or individual cookies;
  • block cookies from specific or all websites;
  • set cookies to be deleted when you close the browser.
