The whole apartment

Apartment renovation types is the starting point if you are renovating the whole apartment: cosmetic and full renovation, turnkey renovation, new-build apartment fit-out, budget renovation, the renovation estimate and the interior design project.

Apartment renovation by building series has pages on the 602, 119, 103, 104, 467 and 464 series, the Lithuanian project, Khrushchyovkas, and Stalin-era, pre-war and wooden buildings. It covers what can be said about each building type from public sources and what has to be checked in the particular apartment.

Rooms and individual finishing work

Room renovation in an apartment covers the bathroom, toilet, bathroom and toilet together, kitchen, rooms, hallway, balcony and loggia. Choose this section if you are renovating one or two rooms.

Renovation work in an apartment is individual finishing and interior work: painting, skimming, tiling, floors, ceilings, demolition, interior doors, wiring, risers, radiators, waterproofing and soundproofing. Most jobs have their own “from” price per m² or per piece, and for some the price is set by estimate.

Replanning and situations

Apartment replanning is demolishing walls, making a door opening, combining a kitchen with a room, and getting approval. It also describes which documents are needed.

Renovation for different situations covers renovation before a sale, in a rented apartment, after a purchase, in an inherited apartment, after flooding, after a fire, and adapting an apartment for older people.

Private houses and businesses

Private house interiors and renovation covers private house renovation, a summer house, an attic, a Līvāni house and sauna finishing. Renovation of business premises covers offices, cafés and restaurants, and stairwells.

Gas work, air conditioner installation and construction waste removal are not part of the renovation estimate; more on the page about us. All the prices in one place: apartment renovation prices.
