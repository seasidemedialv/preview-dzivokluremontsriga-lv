Internal wall insulation

Internal wall insulation is a solution when an apartment has a cold external wall but insulating the building's facade is not planned.

The external wall is common property of the whole building, so work inside the apartment is limited to its inner side, the wall finish. In Riga and the Riga region this work is priced by estimate, because the cost depends on the wall material, the area and the chosen solution.

Why the external wall in an apartment is cold

nopo.lv lists heat loss through the facade among the typical problems of 467 series buildings, and heat loss through the floor slabs for the 103 series. About pre-war masonry buildings, apollo.lv writes that thick walls and high ceilings increase heating costs.

Not every cold or damp wall is a thermal insulation issue. Descriptions of 602 series buildings mention cracking of panel joints, waterproofing of joints, possible moisture penetration and roof leaks, and recommend checking corners for mould. If there are damp stains or mould in a corner, first find out the cause together with the building manager, because the joints and the roof are common property.

Facade or interior: who decides

The Law on Residential Properties counts external walls as common property, and wall finishes inside an apartment as part of the apartment. Insulating the facade is therefore a decision for the whole building, taken by the apartment owners' association. In their own apartment, the owner may work without anyone else's consent as long as the work does not affect common property.

Support is available for the whole building: Altum loans for the renovation of apartment buildings, which since 08.08.2026 the association can take out in its own name, and the Riga municipality initiative “Rīga renovē” with consultations and co-financing for an energy audit, a technical survey and a technical design. These programmes are intended for the whole building. If renovation is already being discussed in your building, ask the building manager before insulating from the inside.

Sources: Law on Residential Properties, Sections 3 and 4, Section 11(1) and (3); LV portāls 08.08.2026 on Altum loans; Riga City Council, LV portāls 26.08.2026 on “Rīga renovē”. Checked 11.11.2026.

What is usually done in an apartment

One solution is a lining on a frame: metal profiles are built against the external wall, thermal insulation material is laid between them and the frame is covered with plasterboard. Which material and thickness to choose depends on the wall material and on whether there is moisture in the wall, and this can be assessed at a survey.

The lining affects everything on this wall. Window openings become deeper, so the reveal finish and window sills have to be redone; see window reveals and sills. Sockets and switches have to be moved to the new surface.

The radiator under the window is usually common property, because it works from the building's heating system. If it has to be removed or moved, this must be agreed with the building manager; see radiator replacement in an apartment. The owner may replace windows in an external wall at their own expense, in agreement with the building manager.

The order of work, as it is usually done

  1. Survey The condition of the wall, signs of moisture and the corners are checked. If there is moisture, the cause is dealt with first.
  2. Agreement If a radiator has to be removed or a window replaced, this is agreed with the building manager.
  3. Electrics Sockets and switches are moved to the position on the new lining.
  4. Frame and insulation Profiles are built against the wall and thermal insulation is laid between them.
  5. Plasterboard and openings The frame is covered, the new reveal finish is made and the window sills are installed.
  6. Finishing The surface is skimmed and painted, and then the radiator is installed back.

What to write in the request

  • Which wall and the room area, the length and height of the wall.
  • The building series or year of construction, if you know it, and the wall material.
  • Whether there is a window, a radiator or sockets on the wall.
  • Whether there is moisture or mould in the corners.
  • Whether renovation is planned for the building.

Frequently asked questions

Can an external wall be insulated from the inside without the association's consent?

Wall finishes in an apartment belong to the apartment, and the owner may work on them without anyone else's consent as long as the work does not affect common property. The external wall itself is common property, so if the plan affects the wall itself, a window or a radiator, the building manager's agreement or the association's consent is needed.

Will insulating from the inside reduce the apartment's share of the common property?

No. The Law on Residential Properties states that the share does not change if the area changes within the apartment's boundaries, including through reconstruction. The room does, however, become smaller by the thickness of the lining.

Is there state or municipal support for internal insulation?

Altum loans and “Rīga renovē” are intended for the renovation of a whole apartment building. They are intended for the whole building.
