Parquet restoration: when sanding helps

Sanding removes the top layer: worn varnish, scratches, stains and small irregularities between parquet pieces. After that the wood is exposed again and can be varnished anew. So restoring parquet by sanding works when the parquet as a whole is holding and the pieces do not move. The same applies to floor sanding in general: a plank floor or a solid wood floor can also be sanded and varnished if the wood is sound.

If some pieces are loose, missing or damaged by moisture, they are first repaired or replaced, and that is a separate job. If under the parquet there are wooden boards and joists that squeak or sag, see wooden floor restoration. Three-layer parquet can be sanded only as far as the thickness of the wood top layer allows, as stated by the manufacturer.

Floor sanding and varnishing in the usual order

  1. Clearing the room The furniture is taken out of the room and the skirting boards are removed if they need replacing or get in the way of sanding.
  2. Inspection Loose pieces and nails or staples that could damage the sanding machine are checked.
  3. Sanding The floor is sanded in several passes from coarser to finer, with the edges and corners done separately.
  4. Gaps Gaps and small damaged spots are filled, then the surface is sanded again.
  5. Varnishing After the dust is removed, varnish is applied in several coats. How long to wait between coats and before walking on the floor is stated by the varnish manufacturer.

What to decide and prepare

  • Varnish or oil, matt or glossy finish. This page is about varnishing; if you want oil, write it in your request.
  • Whether to replace the skirting boards.
  • Where to put the furniture during the work and whether the rooms can be cleared one at a time.
  • Whether you will choose the materials yourself.
  • Whether the same rooms will also have wall painting afterwards: the floor is usually restored after it.

The floor finish is part of the apartment owner's property, and municipalities explain that no construction documentation is needed for purely interior work, such as replacing a floor covering.

Frequently asked questions

How much does parquet sanding and varnishing cost per m²?

From €23/m² for labour. The varnish and other materials are in the estimate separately, and the price changes with the condition of the parquet.

Can three-layer parquet be sanded?

Yes, if the wood top layer is thick enough. How many times a particular parquet can be sanded is stated by the manufacturer; write the product in your request if you know it.

How long after varnishing must you keep off the floor?

This is determined by the varnish manufacturer and by the room temperature and humidity. For the time before walking on the floor and before placing furniture, see the varnish technical data sheet.

Does sanding remove deep cracks and black stains?

Not always. Sanding removes the top layer, but deep moisture stains or cracks may remain. Then the damaged pieces are usually replaced.

Can you live in the apartment during sanding?

The room that is being sanded and varnished cannot be used until the varnish has dried, but the other rooms can. If you will be living in the apartment, write this in your request and say which rooms can be cleared one at a time.

Can a painted plank floor also be sanded?

Yes, a painted plank floor is sanded down to the wood, and then it can be varnished. For more on old plank floors, see the page on wooden floor restoration.
