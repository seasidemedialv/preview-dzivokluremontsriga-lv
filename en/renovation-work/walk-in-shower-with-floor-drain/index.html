Shower with a floor drain - a drain in the floor in place of the bath

A shower with a floor drain in Riga means a shower without a tray: the water runs across a tiled floor to a drain in the floor.

Such a shower can be built in place of the bath when the bathroom is renovated from scratch. Floor drain installation costs from €100 per drain, while the floor slope, waterproofing and tiling are separate jobs.

What to check before replacing a bath with a shower

The drain needs space in the floor and a sewer connection at a height that lets the water run away. In an apartment building the floor sits on a floor slab, and the slabs are the common property of the apartment owners. If the drain would require chasing into the slab, it affects common property and needs the association's consent. That is why the drain and slope are usually formed in the floor build-up above the slab, and the floor level in the bathroom may rise.

How much the floor will rise depends on the drain model and the position of the sewer connection. In your request, describe where the bath's connection and the riser are, and what the door threshold is like.

If the bathroom is above a neighbour's living room, building code LBN 200-21 allows it where the requirements for soundproofing, waterproofing, thermal insulation, fire safety, ventilation and safety are met. If you are moving the bathroom, check whether a construction notice is needed (simplified renovation). For a shower without a tray, waterproofing is the most important layer under the tiles.

Bathrooms and shower rooms in residential buildings are ventilated through natural exhaust ducts (LBN 231-15). When planning the new finish, do not cover the ventilation opening.

Sources: the Law on Residential Properties (Dzīvokļa īpašuma likums), Sections 4 and 11; LBN 200-21, paragraph 136; LBN 231-15, paragraph 90.². Checked 11.11.2026.

Waterproofing around the drain

In the Ceresit CL 51 technical data sheet, the manufacturer recommends at least two coats, so that the dry layer is 0.5 mm. Waterproofing tape is worked into the joints between wall and floor, pressed into the first coat and covered with the second. Sleeves or membranes are used around the drain and pipes, and corner pieces in the corners.

The order of work, as it is usually done

  1. Demolition The bath, the old tiles and, if needed, the old floor layer are removed.
  2. Installing the drain The drain is connected to the sewer connection and fixed at the required height.
  3. Slope The floor layer is formed with a fall towards the drain so that water does not stay in the shower area.
  4. Waterproofing The floor and walls in the shower area are coated in two layers, with tape in the joints and a sleeve around the drain.
  5. Tiling Floor tiles are laid with a fall towards the drain. For Ceresit CM 17, the manufacturer states that the floor is grouted no sooner than after 24 hours.
  6. Joints and silicone The manufacturer requires the joints in corners, at the floor and at the sanitaryware to be filled with silicone, not with grout.

What to decide before your request

  • Type of drain: a point drain in the middle of the shower or a linear drain by the wall.
  • Shower enclosure: a glass screen, a door or a curtain.
  • Whether the washing machine stays in the bathroom and where its connection will be.
  • Whether you need grab rails or a seat. If you are adapting the shower for an older person, see renovation for seniors.
  • Whether you will choose the materials yourself. Write this in your request.

Frequently asked questions

Can a shower with a floor drain be built in an old prefabricated panel building?

It depends on the height of the sewer connection and on how much the floor can be raised, because chasing into the slab affects common property and requires the association's consent. Describe where the sewer connection is.

Can a drain be fitted without changing the whole bathroom floor?

The drain sits in the floor build-up under the tiles, so in practice the whole floor of the shower area has to be redone with slope, waterproofing and tiles. If only the grout joints in the tiles need renewing, see renewing tile grout joints.

When can the new shower be used?

It depends on the materials. For example, for Ceresit CE 40 grout the manufacturer states water load after 24 hours and full strength after 5 days, and for the first 5 days it is cleaned with clean water only.

Does replacing a bath with a shower need approval?

Under the Law on Residential Properties, the bath and shower equipment belongs to the apartment. If risers are moved, load-bearing walls are affected or the slab is chased, the work affects common property and needs the association's consent. If the bathroom is moved or a non-load-bearing wall is removed, check whether a construction notice is needed.
