Laminate is usually a floating floor, so the result is decided by the subfloor: it must be dry and flat enough. In your request, write the floor area of the rooms in m² and what is on the floor now.

What to know about the subfloor before laminate

In the Quick-Step laminate installation instructions, the manufacturer states that subfloor unevenness greater than 2 mm per 1 m must be levelled out. If the floor is more uneven, floor levelling is needed before the laminate. Unevenness can only be seen once the old floor covering has been removed.

Moisture is the second question. The same manufacturer requires a concrete subfloor moisture content below 2.5% (CM method) under laminate, and below 1.5% with underfloor heating. For a new concrete floor, Quick-Step states that it dries for at least one week per centimetre of thickness up to 4 cm, and twice as long when thicker. Limits differ between manufacturers, so check the instructions for your laminate.

Source: Quick-Step laminate installation instructions LV. Checked 11.11.2026.

Laminate installation in the usual order

  1. Acclimatisation Quick-Step recommends keeping the panels in unopened packaging for 48 hours at room temperature in the room where they will be laid.
  2. Removing the old floor covering Old linoleum, carpet or laminate flooring is removed if demolition is in the estimate.
  3. Subfloor The subfloor must be flat and dry according to the laminate manufacturer's requirements; if it is not, it is levelled.
  4. Underlay The underlay is laid on a dry, flat subfloor.
  5. Laying the panels Quick-Step requires an expansion gap of at least 8–10 mm along all edges, around pipes, thresholds and under doors; the hole for a pipe must be 20 mm larger than the pipe.
  6. Skirting boards and thresholds The gaps at the walls are covered with skirting boards, and thresholds are fitted in door openings.

What to decide before installation

  • Whether the laminate will run in one piece through several rooms. In large spaces, Quick-Step provides for an expansion gap every 13 m.
  • Whether there will be underfloor heating under the laminate. Quick-Step states that the surface of a heated subfloor must not be warmer than 28.8 °C.
  • What the skirting boards will be, and whether they are fitted straight away or after the walls are painted.
  • Whether you will choose the materials yourself. If the laminate manufacturer is already known, write it in your request.
  • Whether the doors will need to be trimmed after the new floor covering.

Replacing a floor covering is interior work, and municipalities explain that it needs no construction documentation. If you are choosing between laminate and another covering, see vinyl flooring installation and parquet installation.

Frequently asked questions

How much does laminate installation cost per square metre?

From €6.50/m² for labour, including laying the underlay. The laminate, floor levelling and skirting boards are separate.

Can laminate be laid over old linoleum?

The subfloor must be flat and stable, and the laminate manufacturer's instructions usually state which subfloors it may be laid on.

How long should you wait after a new screed before laying laminate?

Quick-Step states that a new concrete floor dries for at least one week per centimetre of thickness up to 4 cm, and the moisture content under laminate must be below 2.5% (CM). It is better to measure the moisture than to calculate by days alone.

Can laminate be laid on underfloor heating?

Quick-Step allows it if the subfloor moisture content is below 1.5% (CM) and the surface is not warmer than 28.8 °C. For other manufacturers, see their instructions.
