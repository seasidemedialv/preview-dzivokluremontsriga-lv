Parquet is wood, so it matters how dry the floor beneath it is. In the request, write the type of parquet, the floor area of the rooms in m² and what is on the floor now.

Three-layer parquet or solid wood parquet

The usual choice is between three-layer parquet (layers glued in the factory, with a wooden top layer) and solid wood parquet or parquet boards. How a particular product is laid, floating or glued, and what base it needs, is set by the parquet manufacturer's instructions. So write the product in the request if it has already been chosen.

If the parquet comes from the factory already varnished or oiled, it is usually not sanded after installation. If the parquet is untreated, parquet sanding and varnishing is needed after installation, and it is a separate line in the estimate.

Base moisture under parquet

In the technical data sheet of the Ceresit CN 76 self-levelling compound, the manufacturer states that under parquet and wooden floor coverings the moisture of a cement base can be up to 2.0% without heating and up to 1.8% with underfloor heating. For a gypsum base the limit is 0.5%, and 0.3% with heating.

If the floor has to be levelled before the parquet, the same manufacturer states drying of 4 mm per day for a layer up to 1.5 cm and 3 mm per day for a thicker one (+23 °C, 50% humidity). So after floor levelling, parquet is laid only when a measurement shows the permitted moisture, not by the calendar.

Source: Ceresit CN 76 technical data sheet. Limits may differ for other manufacturers. Checked 11.11.2026.

Laying a parquet floor in the usual order

  1. Removing the old covering The old covering is removed down to the base if demolition is in the estimate.
  2. Base The base must be level and dry according to the parquet manufacturer's requirements; if it is not, it is levelled and left to dry.
  3. Parquet in the room Before installation, the parquet is kept in the room for as long as the manufacturer requires.
  4. Installation The parquet is laid according to the manufacturer's instructions, floating on an underlay or glued, with gaps at the walls.
  5. Finishing Untreated parquet is sanded and varnished, after which the skirting boards and thresholds are fitted.

What to decide before parquet

  • The type and pattern of the parquet: straight rows, “herringbone” or another pattern changes the amount of work.
  • Whether there will be underfloor heating under the parquet: then check whether the manufacturer allows it.
  • Whether the parquet is factory-finished or will be varnished after installation.
  • Whether you will choose the materials yourself.
  • Whether the doors need trimming after the new covering.

Replacing the floor covering in an apartment is interior work, and municipalities explain that no construction documentation is needed for it. If you are still choosing, compare it with laminate installation.

How much parquet installation costs

Parquet installation: from €25/m². Parquet sanding and varnishing: from €23/m². Floor levelling, poured compound or screed: from €6.50/m². Prices are for labour. Materials are calculated separately in the estimate.

Example: a 20 m² room, installation 20 × €25 = from €500 for labour. If the parquet is not factory-finished, sanding and varnishing is added, 20 × €23 = from €460, in total from €960.

The price changes with the floor area and number of rooms, the condition of the floor, and the type and pattern of the parquet. All prices are on the page renovation prices. An exact estimate can be made after seeing the apartment.

Frequently asked questions

How much does three-layer parquet installation cost?

Parquet installation costs from €25/m² for labour. If the three-layer parquet is factory-varnished, sanding is usually not needed; materials are in the estimate separately.

Can parquet be laid on underfloor heating?

Some manufacturers allow it. The Ceresit CN 76 technical data sheet gives up to 1.8% for the moisture of a cement base with heating. Check the parquet manufacturer's instructions to see whether your parquet is suitable for underfloor heating.

How long do you have to wait after floor levelling?

It depends on the layer thickness, temperature and humidity. Parquet is laid when a measurement shows the moisture the manufacturer permits.

Can parquet be laid in the kitchen or the hallway?

It can, if the parquet manufacturer allows it for such rooms. If you are unsure, see the comparison in the advice article on tiles and laminate.

Is a permit needed to replace parquet?

Municipalities explain that no construction documentation is needed to replace a floor covering in an apartment. If walls or pipes are rebuilt together with the floor, check whether a construction notice is needed.
