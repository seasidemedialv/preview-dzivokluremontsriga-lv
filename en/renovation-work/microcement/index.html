Microcement application - microcement for floors, walls and showers

Microcement application in Riga and the Riga region is a finish for a floor, walls or shower area, applied in several layers and covered with a protective lacquer.

When to choose microcement

Microcement is chosen when you want a surface without tile joints, where the wall and floor can look like one area. The look of microcement depends on the work: the tone and pattern differ slightly in each place, so before the work it is worth seeing a sample and agreeing the tone.

If you want texture only on a dry wall, compare it with decorative plaster application, which has a price per m².

Substrate and wet zone

Microcement is applied to a stable, flat substrate. If the floor is uneven, floor levelling is needed first (from €6.50/m²). Whether to apply it over old tiles or remove them is decided by the condition of the substrate and the material manufacturer's instructions; demolition of old tiles costs from €9/m².

In a shower area and bathroom, the estimate also includes waterproofing: waterproofing in a bathroom is a separate line, from €6/m². Read more on the page waterproofing in a bathroom. If the whole room has to be redone, see bathroom renovation.

Order of work

  1. Substrate check The substrate is checked for stability, flatness, loose tiles and cracks.
  2. Preparation If needed, the old tiles are removed, the floor is levelled and primed.
  3. Waterproofing In a wet zone, waterproofing is installed before the decorative surface.
  4. Microcement layers The layers are applied according to the manufacturer's instructions, observing the time between them.
  5. Protective lacquer The surface is covered with a protective lacquer; the number of coats is specified by the manufacturer.

What to decide before your request

  • which surfaces: floor, walls or shower;
  • tone and pattern;
  • whether the old tiles are removed;
  • whether you will choose the materials yourself.

In the form, write the address, the surface areas in m² and the current substrate.

Microcement or tiles in a bathroom

Tiling has a price per m²: from €24/m². Example for a bathroom: tiling 18 m² × €24 = from €432, waterproofing 5 m² × €6 = from €30 and floor drain installation from €100, in total from €562 (these three jobs only). For microcement, similar preparation and waterproofing lines come into the estimate, but the surface itself is by estimate.

The choice is usually a matter of look and upkeep. With tiles, the grout joints need care; with microcement, the manufacturer's instructions on the protective lacquer and cleaning products must be followed. For tiles in wet rooms, see bathroom tiling.

How much microcement costs

Microcement application: price by estimate. It is determined by:

  • the surface area in m² and whether it is a floor, wall or shower;
  • the condition of the substrate and the preparation;
  • the number of layers and the protective lacquer;

Prices are for labour. Materials are calculated separately in the estimate. Example: in a shower area, 5 m² of waterproofing, 5 × €6 = from €30; the microcement itself is by estimate.

Frequently asked questions

Can microcement be applied over old tiles?

This is decided by the condition of the tiles and the material manufacturer's instructions. Loose or cracked tiles are removed.

Is microcement suitable for a shower?

It can be used in a shower area if there is waterproofing under it and the material is intended for wet rooms. This is checked in the manufacturer's technical data sheet.

Can microcement be applied in a kitchen over a worktop?

It can, if the material manufacturer intends it for surfaces exposed to water and grease. In your request, write the dimensions of the surface.

Why is there no price per m² for microcement?

The scope of work varies greatly with the substrate, the location and the number of layers. That is why the price is by estimate.
