Demolition of walls, floors and finishes costs from €6/m², and demolition of old tiles from €9/m². Before the job you need to know what belongs to your apartment and what belongs to the whole building.

What you may demolish in an apartment and what belongs to the building

The Law on Residential Properties (Dzīvokļa īpašuma likums) counts as part of the apartment the non-load-bearing internal partitions, the finishes of ceilings, floors and walls, doors, utility lines up to the common-property risers, and the kitchen, toilet, shower and bathroom equipment. These parts can be removed if the work does not affect common property or other apartments.

The common property of the building includes load-bearing walls and columns, walls between apartments, floor slabs with their thermal and sound insulation, external walls, windows in external structures and the building's utility systems. If the work affects them, you need the consent of the apartment owners' association. A riser is common property, so removing it needs a decision of the apartment owners' association, and the building manager organises the work; more on the page riser replacement in an apartment.

Law on Residential Properties, Section 3(2), Section 4(1) paragraph 1, Section 11(1) and (3). Checked 11.11.2026.

Removing a partition wall: when you need documents

If you remove only finishes, such as wallpaper, tiles or a floor covering, the municipalities explain that no construction documents are needed. If you remove a non-load-bearing wall to merge rooms, according to the Riga municipality's explanation a certified architect prepares a plan and a construction notice is submitted in the Construction Information System (BIS) as simplified renovation. If the wall separates two apartments, you need the consent of both owners.

An opening in a load-bearing wall is a simplified reconstruction: you need a solution from a certified architect and a structural engineer, an explanatory memorandum and, if the building is divided into apartment properties, the consent of more than half of the apartment owners. Only the building's design or a structural engineer's opinion shows which walls are load-bearing. More on the page removing walls in an apartment.

Cabinet Regulation No. 529 (“Ēku būvnoteikumi”, Building Construction Regulations), sub-paragraph 7.1 and paragraph 8 of 7.²; reply of the Riga municipality on LV portāls, 04.12.2025. Checked 11.11.2026.

Older buildings: asbestos

In buildings built before the asbestos ban (EU from 01.01.2005), some materials can contain asbestos, for example pipe insulation or vinyl floor tiles and their adhesive. You cannot identify it reliably by appearance; it is determined in a laboratory. Asbestos is dangerous if it is sawn, drilled or otherwise damaged, and work with it is regulated by Cabinet Regulation No. 852. Asbestos may be removed only with a permit from the State Labour Inspectorate. More on the page asbestos in apartments.

Demolition in the usual order

  1. List Before the job you need to know what is removed and what stays: walls, floors, tiles, doors, plumbing, ceilings.
  2. Electricity and water Electricity and water are switched off in the zone being demolished. If a riser or heating has to be switched off, this is agreed with the building manager.
  3. Fittings and doors Plumbing, kitchen fittings, doors and skirting boards are removed.
  4. Finishes and tiles Tiles, plaster, wallpaper and ceiling finishes are removed down to a firm base.
  5. Floors The floor covering is removed and, if planned, the old screed, without damaging the floor slab.
  6. Partition walls Non-load-bearing partitions are removed only when the required documents are in order.

What to prepare before demolition

  • Take out or pack up the furniture and belongings that stay in the apartment.
  • Ask the building manager whether the building has set renovation hours. The apartment owners' association can set house rules.
  • Warn your neighbours. Disturbing a person's peace is an administrative offence, but the Riga Municipal Police explain that everyday noise is usually not an offence.
  • Think about the waste. For construction waste removal see buvgruzuizvesanariga.lv.
  • In your request, write what to remove, how many m², and the building series or year.

Frequently asked questions

Do you need a permit to remove old tiles?

The municipalities explain that no construction documents are needed to remove finishes. Documents are needed if a wall is removed or load-bearing structures are affected.

Is construction waste removal included in the price?

No. The demolition price is for labour. Construction waste removal is arranged separately.

How do I know whether a wall is load-bearing?

Only the building's design or a structural engineer's opinion shows which walls are load-bearing. If you have the apartment plan, you can send it to the specialist when they contact you.

Can an old floor contain asbestos?

Vinyl floor tiles and their adhesive in older buildings can contain asbestos. You cannot identify it by appearance; it is determined in a laboratory.
