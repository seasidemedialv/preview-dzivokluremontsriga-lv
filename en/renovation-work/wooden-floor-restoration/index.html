Wooden floors in old Riga houses

In pre-war masonry buildings the floor slabs are mostly wooden, and Riga also has many wooden apartment houses, for example in Āgenskalns, Čiekurkalns, Maskavas forštate and Grīziņkalns. In such apartments there may be a plank floor on timber structures beneath the existing covering. How old it is and in what condition can only be seen once the covering has been removed.

An important boundary: the floor finish is the apartment owner's part, but the floor slabs with their heat and sound insulation are the common property of the apartment owners. If the work affects only the boards and the covering, it is a renovation of your apartment. If beams have to be replaced or reinforced, it affects common property, and then the consent of the apartment owners' association is needed.

Sources: apollo.lv on pre-war houses; apkaimes.lv and deepbaltic.com on wooden houses; Law on Residential Properties, Sections 3, 4 and 11. Checked 11.11.2026.

Painted boards are sanded down to the wood before varnishing. If the floor is levelled so that a new covering can be laid on it, check in the levelling material manufacturer's instructions that it is intended for a wooden base. For a new covering, parquet installation or laminate installation are also suitable.

The work in the usual order

  1. Condition of the boards The boards are checked for which ones move, creak or are damaged, and for signs of moisture beneath them.
  2. Removing the old covering If there is linoleum or another covering on the boards, it is removed.
  3. Fixing and replacing boards Loose boards are fixed down and damaged ones are replaced.
  4. Finishing The boards are sanded and varnished or prepared for a new covering.
  5. Skirting boards Skirting boards and thresholds are installed.

What to decide and write in the request

  • The year or type of the building: pre-war masonry building, wooden house or other.
  • What is wrong with the floor: creaking, moving, uneven, worn, painted.
  • What result you want: exposed wood or a new covering.
  • The area of the rooms in m² and the condition of the floor, including in corners and by thresholds.
  • Whether you will choose the materials yourself.

If the whole floor structure has to be replaced down to the beams, this is usually part of a larger job: see renovation of an apartment in a pre-war building or renovation of an apartment in a wooden house.

Frequently asked questions

What should I do if a wooden floor creaks?

Creaking usually means that the boards are moving. They are fixed down or replaced, and only then is the floor sanded or a new covering laid. If the beams are moving, it affects the common property of the building.

Can an old plank floor be sanded and varnished?

Yes, if the boards are sound and thick enough. Painted boards are sanded down to the wood. The nearest price for sanding and varnishing is from €23/m².

Do I need to ask the neighbours about replacing beams?

Floor slabs are the common property of the apartment owners, so work that affects beams needs the consent of the apartment owners' association. Start by asking the building manager.
