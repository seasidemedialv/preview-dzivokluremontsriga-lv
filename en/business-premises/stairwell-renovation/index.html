Stairwell renovation in Riga

Stairwell renovation in an apartment building in Riga is work in common property: it is decided by the apartment owners' association and organised by the building manager.

Who decides on a stairwell renovation

Shared premises, including the stairwell, are the common property of the apartment owners (Law on Residential Properties, Section 4). Work in common property needs the association's consent, and a decision is taken if the owners who represent more than half of the apartment properties in the building vote for it, unless the law requires more.

Each owner pays the building's management costs according to their share of common property, on the basis of the association's decision. The share is the ratio of the apartment area to the total area of all the apartments. Routine repair of common property is one of the mandatory management activities (Law on the Management of Residential Buildings, Section 6), so start with the building manager.

Law on Residential Properties, Sections 4, 5, 11, 13 and 16; Law on the Management of Residential Buildings (Dzīvojamo māju pārvaldīšanas likums), Section 6(2). Checked 11.11.2026.

What a stairwell renovation includes

  • Filling cracks and damage, skimming walls and ceilings.
  • Painting walls and ceilings on all floors and at the entrance.
  • Floors at the entrance and on the landings: tiling or restoring the existing covering.
  • Interior doors and vestibule doors, if they are not external structures of the building.
  • Light points and switches; electricity supply for common property is a management job, so the building manager organises it.

An owner must treat common property with care and comply with fire safety and other requirements (Law on Residential Properties, Section 10).

Accessibility: a lift or a ramp

To ensure accessibility, an owner, after informing the association or the building manager in advance, may install a lift in the stairwell, build a ramp or otherwise adapt common property (Law on Residential Properties, Section 11(5)). The Riga municipality's benefit for adapting a home does not include adapting shared premises in apartment buildings. More: adapting an apartment for seniors.

The order of work in a stairwell

  1. Decision The association takes a decision on the work and its financing; the building manager prepares the proposal for the vote.
  2. List of work The list of work contains: m² of walls and ceilings, m² of floors, doors and light points.
  3. Informing residents The building may have its own rules of use; ask the building manager whether they include renovation times.
  4. Walls and ceilings Filling cracks, skimming, priming and painting from the top floor down.
  5. Floors and doors Tiles or covering, then doors and light fittings.

Frequently asked questions

Can one apartment owner order a stairwell renovation?

The stairwell is common property, so the work needs the association's consent. A decision is taken if the owners who represent more than half of the apartment properties vote for it. Start with the building manager.

How are the costs of a stairwell renovation shared?

Each owner pays the costs according to their share of common property, on the basis of the association's decision. The share is the ratio of the apartment area to the total area of all the apartments.

How much does a stairwell renovation cost?

It depends on the area of walls and ceilings. For example, 400 m² of skimming and painting is from €5,600 for labour. An exact estimate can be made after seeing the building.

Can a building manager request a stairwell renovation?
