Frequently asked questions

Do these articles replace an answer from the building board or a lawyer?

No. The articles explain the rules in general; for a specific apartment the decision is made by the building board, and in disputes a lawyer is useful.

Why is there a date next to the rules?

Rules change, for example the current Fire Safety Regulations cease to be in force on 02.01.2027. The date shows when the source was last checked.
