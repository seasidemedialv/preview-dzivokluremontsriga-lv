When renovation work is allowed in an apartment

When you may carry out renovation work in Riga is not determined by one schedule of hours for all apartments. In Riga's rules on territory maintenance and building upkeep currently in force, requirements on the time of renovation work or on noise are not set. What decides it is the law on disturbing public order and your building's house rules, if the apartment owners' association has adopted any.

What the law says

The Administrative Penalties Law (public order) provides liability for disturbing public order where a person's peace is disturbed. A private individual can be given a warning or fined up to 100 fine units, that is, up to €500.

The law does not tie this to a particular job, such as drilling or chiselling. What matters is the result: whether the noise disturbs other people's peace.

Sources: Administrative Penalties Law (public order), Section 11(1); Administrative Liability Law (Administratīvās atbildības likums), Section 16(2) (1 fine unit = €5). Checked 11.11.2026.

Noise rules in Riga: what the municipal police say

In June 2026, the Riga Municipal Police explained that making noise is not in itself directly prohibited. Liability arises if public order is disturbed, and everyday domestic noise is usually not a violation.

The police do not measure noise levels in residential premises. If a neighbour's noise is disturbing you now, the police advise calling the municipal police so that it is checked on the spot.

In Riga's rules on territory maintenance and building upkeep currently in force (RD-24-270-sn, 15.05.2024), requirements on the time of renovation work or on noise are not set. The earlier Riga rule that required noisy work to be agreed with the building manager is no longer in force.

Sources: Riga Municipal Police explanation on LV portāls 12.06.2026; Riga City Council binding regulations RD-24-270-sn. Checked 11.11.2026.

House rules

The Law on Residential Properties (Dzīvokļa īpašuma likums) allows the apartment owners' association to set house rules and restrictions, and owners must comply with them. They can also include renovation hours. So before noisy work, ask the building manager whether renovation hours have been adopted for your building.

In the Riga region the situation can differ between municipalities. If the apartment is, for example, in Mārupe, Ķekava or Jūrmala, ask the building manager and the municipality what applies to your building.

Source: Law on Residential Properties (Dzīvokļa īpašuma likums), Section 10(1) points 6 and 9. Checked 11.11.2026.

Renovation work at weekends

At weekends and in the evenings the assessment is the same: whether the noise disturbs other people's peace and whether the house rules contain restrictions adopted by the association.

If it is not clear before the renovation what is acceptable in your building, the safest course is to agree with the neighbours and the building manager in advance rather than argue afterwards.

How to plan noisy work

  1. Ask the building manager Whether the building has association rules on renovation hours, how the water is shut off and where materials may be placed in shared areas.
  2. Warn the neighbours A notice by the stairwell door or a conversation: which days there will be demolition and chiselling, and how long it will continue.
  3. Group the noisier work Demolition, removing old tiles, chasing for wires and pipes and drilling into concrete usually make the most noise. If they are done one after another, the noisy stage is shorter.
  4. Respect common property The stairwell is a shared area. The Law on Residential Properties requires careful treatment of common property.

When can you drill? By the same logic: if drilling does not disturb others' peace and the house rules do not restrict it, a separate ban by clock time is not set in Riga's rules. If neighbours ask you to spare them at a certain time, that is usually cheaper than a dispute.

If a neighbour is making noise

  • Talk to the neighbour first: it may be enough to find out how long the work will continue.
  • Ask the building manager whether house rules on renovation hours have been adopted for the building.
  • If the noise is disturbing you now, the Riga Municipal Police advise calling the police so that it is checked on the spot.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a quiet time for renovation in Riga?

In Riga's rules on territory maintenance and building upkeep currently in force, requirements on the time of renovation work are not set. Liability is for disturbing the peace, and the apartment owners' association can set its own renovation hours.

Is renovation work at weekends prohibited?

Look for the answer in your building's house rules, if the association has adopted them. Otherwise the assessment is the same as on weekdays: whether the noise disturbs other people's peace.

What penalty is there for making noise?

For disturbing public order by disturbing a person's peace, a private individual: a warning or a fine of up to 100 fine units (up to €500).

Will the municipal police come to measure the noise?

The Riga Municipal Police do not measure noise levels in residential premises. They advise calling while the noise continues so that the situation is checked on the spot.
