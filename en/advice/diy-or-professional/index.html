Renovating yourself or with a tradesperson?

Renovating yourself or with a tradesperson is a choice made work by work, not for the whole apartment at once. Painting, wallpapering and replacing the floor covering can be done by yourself and need no documents. For electrical wiring, gas, risers and demolishing walls there are rules that require specialists, consent or documents.

What you can do yourself without documents

For interior work that does not involve renovation, reconstruction or demolition, construction intent documents are not needed (Cabinet Regulation No. 529, sub-paragraph 7.1). The municipality explains that painting walls and ceilings, wallpapering and replacing the floor covering need no documentation.

An owner may carry out construction work in their apartment without the consent of other owners, observing the regulations, insofar as the work does not affect common property or other apartments (Law on Residential Properties, Section 11).

Sources: Cabinet Regulation No. 529 of 02.09.2014, sub-paragraph 7.1; explanation of Liepāja State City Municipality on LV portāls 11.03.2026; Law on Residential Properties, Section 11(1). Checked 11.11.2026.

Where the rules require a specialist or consent

Work What the rules require
Installing electrical wiring Carried out under the supervision of a certified construction specialist; inspections are carried out by a certified electrical specialist (LBN 261-15)
Replacing or moving a gas hob within one room The user may do so in agreement with the gas supplier, if combustion products are not discharged into a flue; an appliance with a flue needs the supplier's permission and a flue and ventilation duct certificate (LBN 241-15)
Moving a gas appliance to another room The supplier's technical conditions, a flue and ventilation certificate and a building design (LBN 241-15)
Risers, radiators that depend on the building's heating Common property: the consent of the apartment owners' association is needed (Law on Residential Properties)
Demolishing a non-load-bearing wall A plan by a certified architect and a construction notice in BIS (Riga municipality's explanation)
An opening in a load-bearing wall An architect and a structural engineer with certificates, an explanatory memorandum (Riga municipality's explanation)

For simplified renovation or simplified reconstruction, a builder who does the work personally does not need the right of independent practice in construction (Cabinet Regulation No. 529, sub-paragraph 107.2). This does not mean that documents are not needed: the construction notice is accompanied by a plan of the room group, prepared and confirmed in BIS by a construction company or a construction specialist.

Sources: Cabinet Regulation No. 294, LBN 261-15, paragraph 55; Cabinet Regulation No. 336, LBN 241-15, paragraphs 7–9; Law on Residential Properties, Sections 4 and 11; Riga municipality's reply on LV portāls 04.12.2025; Cabinet Regulation No. 529, sub-paragraphs 22.1 and 107.2. Checked 11.11.2026.

Bathroom renovation yourself

In a bathroom a mistake may come to light later, because water under tiles is not visible, and it can reach the neighbour. An apartment owner is liable for losses caused to other owners.

Manufacturers' requirements show how precise this work is. For Ceresit CL 51 waterproofing the manufacturer recommends at least two coats up to 0.5 mm of dry layer, tapes at the joints and collars at pipes and drains. Ceresit CM 17 adhesive must cover at least 65% of the underside of the tile, and in corners and next to plumbing fixtures the joints are filled with silicone. If any of the steps is skipped, the mistake stays under the tiles.

If only a toilet or a tap is changing in the bathroom, that is a plumber's job; see santehnikisriga.lv.

Sources: Law on Residential Properties, Section 14(1); Ceresit CL 51 and CM 17 technical data sheets (Henkel). Checked 11.11.2026.

If you choose a tradesperson or a company

  1. Check the register If the contractor provides construction work, check them in the Register of Construction Merchants: a company providing construction services must be registered in it. The register is part of BIS (bis.gov.lv) and is available free of charge.
  2. Agree in writing A contract for work concluded orally can be valid, but the customer has to prove the contract and the payment. Write the list of work and the price into the estimate or the contract.
  3. Know your rights A consumer can make a claim for non-conformity with the contract within two years of receiving the service, and the claim must be submitted within two months of discovering the non-conformity.
  4. In case of a dispute A consumer can ask the Consumer Rights Protection Centre (PTAC) or the Consumer Disputes Resolution Commission to assess the service provider's conduct.

More in the articles Register of Construction Merchants: how to check a builder and renovation contract: what it must contain.

Sources: Construction Law, Section 22; Cabinet Regulation No. 116 of 25.02.2014; LV portāls, e-consultation 26.11.2025; Consumer Rights Protection Law, Section 27(1). Checked 11.11.2026.

Frequently asked questions

Does a DIY apartment renovation have to be notified?

Painting, wallpaper and replacing the floor covering need no documentation. If you are reconstructing walls or affecting common property, check whether a notice and the association's consent are needed.

May I install the electrical wiring myself?

LBN 261-15 states that installation is carried out under the supervision of a certified construction specialist, and inspections are carried out by a certified electrical specialist.

May I move a gas hob myself?

Within one room, the user may do so in agreement with the gas supplier, if the hob has no flue. An appliance with a flue needs the supplier's permission and a flue and ventilation duct certificate even in the same room. For another room, the supplier's technical conditions, a flue and ventilation certificate and a building design are needed.

Can I order only part of the work?

Yes. In the request, write which work you will do yourself. After you submit the form, we contact you within 5 minutes.
