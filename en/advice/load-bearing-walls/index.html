Can you demolish a wall in an apartment

Whether you can demolish a wall in an apartment depends on whether the wall is load-bearing. A non-load-bearing partition may be demolished by submitting a construction notice in Riga with a plan prepared by an architect. A load-bearing wall is the common property of the building: an opening in it needs a structural engineer's solution, an explanatory memorandum and the consent of more than half of the apartment owners.

Non-load-bearing wall: what to do

Non-load-bearing internal partitions belong to the apartment, not to common property. Demolishing one to combine rooms is simplified renovation: a functional improvement that does not change the load-bearing structures, the volume or the use.

  1. Plan Riga municipality explains that an architect with a construction practice certificate prepares a plan of the group of rooms with the changes and an explication with the room heights.
  2. Notice in BIS Before the work starts, a construction notice is submitted in BIS as simplified renovation.
  3. Neighbour, if the wall is between apartments If a non-load-bearing wall separates two apartments, the owners of both apartments agree; the consent of the other owners is not needed.
  4. Cadastral file After the work, the owners update the cadastral survey file.

Sources: Law on Residential Properties, Section 3(2); Cabinet Regulation No. 529, paragraph 1.¹ sub-paragraph 5, paragraphs 7.¹ and 22; Riga municipality explanation on LV portāls, 04.12.2025. Checked 11.11.2026.

Load-bearing wall: an opening or full demolition

Load-bearing walls and columns, walls between apartments and floor slabs are the common property of the apartment owners. Making a new opening (door, window, shaft) in a load-bearing wall, or enlarging an existing one, is simplified reconstruction.

  • You need an architect and a structural engineer with a construction practice certificate: the survey, the calculations and the solution for the opening.
  • An explanatory memorandum is submitted to the building board, with the structural details of the opening and an assessment of the effect on the strength and stability of the building.
  • If the building is divided into apartment properties, the consent of more than half (50% + 1) of the apartment owners is needed, recorded as a general meeting decision or a voting record.
  • The work may only start after the building board's mark that the conditions for starting construction work have been met.

For work on load-bearing structures that does not fit the case of an opening, the Building Construction Regulations require a building design in minimum scope and a building permit. If the building is a cultural monument, the solution is also agreed with the National Heritage Board (Nacionālā kultūras mantojuma pārvalde) or the cultural heritage unit of the City Development Department (Pilsētas attīstības departaments).

Sources: Law on Residential Properties, Section 4(1) paragraph 1 and Section 16(3); Cabinet Regulation No. 529, paragraphs 1.¹ 6, 7.² 8, 7.³, 9, 24.4.2, 24.4.3 and 108; Riga municipality explanation on LV portāls, 04.12.2025. Checked 11.11.2026.

How to tell whether a wall is load-bearing

Which walls are load-bearing can be told from the building's design or from a structural engineer's opinion, because it is the engineer who assesses the effect of an opening on the strength of the building.

  • Ask the building manager whether the design drawings for the building are available.
  • Compare the apartment with the plan in the cadastral survey file to see whether the walls have already been changed.
  • If you plan an opening or demolition, a survey by the structural engineer will be needed anyway.

A series description helps you understand what to expect, but the decision on a specific wall is made from the design or the structural engineer's opinion.

The structure differs in series buildings. Publicly available series descriptions give the following, but in a specific apartment it must be checked against the design or the structural engineer's opinion.

What happens if a wall is demolished without documents

If a building inspector finds unauthorised construction, they stop the work, and the authority decides on restoring the previous condition or on a permit after the requirements are met. The owner covers the costs. For work without an explanatory memorandum, a private individual faces a warning or a fine of up to 400 fine units (up to €2,000). More: replanning without approval.

Source: Construction Law, Sections 18 and 25; Administrative Liability Law (Administratīvās atbildības likums), Section 16. Checked 11.11.2026.

Frequently asked questions

Can the wall between the kitchen and a room be demolished?

If it is non-load-bearing, in Riga a construction notice is submitted with an architect's plan. If it is load-bearing, you need a structural engineer's solution for the opening, an explanatory memorandum and the consent of more than half of the apartment owners. With a gas hob, also check the gas rules. More: kitchen combined with a room.

Can rooms be combined in a 602 series building?

Series descriptions state that in the 602 series the internal walls are usually load-bearing, so combining rooms usually means an opening in a load-bearing wall. A structural engineer assesses it, and an explanatory memorandum and association consent are needed.

Do the neighbours have to agree to a wall being demolished?

For a non-load-bearing wall inside your own apartment, other owners' consent is not needed. If the wall separates two apartments, the owners of both apartments agree. An opening in a load-bearing wall needs the consent of more than half of the apartment owners.

Can a door opening be moved along a wall?

Making a new opening in a load-bearing wall is simplified reconstruction with an explanatory memorandum. In a non-load-bearing partition it is usually simplified renovation with a construction notice. More: making a door opening.
