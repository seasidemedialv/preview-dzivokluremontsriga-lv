How to choose a renovation company

How do you choose a renovation company? Compare not the totals but estimates drawn up against the same list of work, check the company in the Register of Construction Merchants and agree in writing. The same approach works if you choose an individual renovation worker: a clear list of work, a price per unit, a deadline and who handles the documents, if they are needed.

Start with your own list of work

If you describe the renovation differently to three companies, you will get three estimates that cannot be compared. Before asking for quotes, write one list: rooms, area in m², wall area in m², what is being demolished, whether the electrical wiring and pipes are changed, what the floor covering and tiles will be. Send the same list to everyone.

If you do not know whether you need a cosmetic or a full renovation, start with this: cosmetic or full renovation.

What to compare in estimates

What to look at Why it matters
Work with quantities and a unit price (m², pcs) you see what you are paying for and can recalculate if the quantity changes
Materials separate from labour you can compare the labour price and decide whether you choose the materials yourself
What is not included construction waste, documents, buying plumbing fixtures and extra work may be left outside the total
Preparation work tiling without levelling and waterproofing, or painting without skimming, are different jobs
Deadline and stages if no deadline is agreed, the service is provided within a reasonable time, and a reasonable time is hard to prove
Company details the company name and registration number allow it to be checked in the register

A very low total may mean that some work has not been written in. Ask what exactly is included in the price and write the answer into the estimate. On how an estimate is built: renovation estimate.

Source: Consumer Rights Protection Law, Section 30(4). Checked 11.11.2026.

Questions to ask before the contract

  • Is the company in the Register of Construction Merchants? You can check this free of charge in the Construction Information System (BIS).
  • How will payment be made: in stages, materials separately, by invoice?
  • Who handles the construction notice or the explanatory memorandum if you change walls or openings? The room group plan is prepared and approved in the BIS by a construction company or construction specialist.
  • Who will do the electrical wiring? The installation is carried out under the direction of a construction specialist, and the checks are made by a certified electrician; when the wiring is rebuilt, residual current protection is needed.
  • How will the work be agreed where it involves risers or radiators, which are common property?
  • How will the work be accepted and how will defects be put right?

Sources: Cabinet Regulation No. 116; Cabinet Regulation No. 529, sub-paragraph 22.1; LBN 261-15, paragraph 55; Ministry of Economics on LV portāls 02.07.2025; Law on Residential Properties (Dzīvokļa īpašuma likums), Section 4. Checked 11.11.2026.

Warning signs

  • They refuse to give an estimate or a contract in writing.
  • They say no documents are needed for an opening in a load-bearing wall. For work without an explanatory memorandum, a private individual can be fined up to 400 fine units (up to €2,000), and the apartment owner is liable.
  • They recommend boxing in the riser without an access hatch. The owner must allow specialists authorised by the building manager access to the building's utilities.
  • They ask for a large advance in cash without a receipt. In a dispute, you will have to prove the payment. More: renovation advance payment.

Sources: Construction Law (Būvniecības likums), Section 25; Administrative Liability Law (Administratīvās atbildības likums), Section 16; Law on Residential Properties (Dzīvokļa īpašuma likums), Section 10; LZAK explanation on LV portāls 26.11.2025. Checked 11.11.2026.

How to choose a renovation worker for one job

If you need only one job, for example tiling or laminate installation, compare the price per m² and what it includes. The price of tiling usually does not include levelling, waterproofing and demolition.

Ask how the worker will follow the material manufacturer's requirements. For example, the Ceresit CM 17 sheet requires the adhesive to cover at least 65% of the underside of the tile, and the Quick-Step instructions require an expansion gap of at least 8–10 mm at the edges of the laminate. If the answer is vague, that is a warning signal.

Sources: Ceresit CM 17 technical data sheet; Quick-Step laminate installation instructions LV. Checked 11.11.2026.

For comparing prices

Prices are for labour. Materials are calculated separately in the estimate. The public prices on this site can be used as a reference point: cosmetic renovation from €100/m², full renovation from €280/m², tiling from €24/m², laminate installation from €6.50/m².

Example: a 2-room apartment of 50 m², cosmetic renovation: 50 × €100 = from €5,000 (labour). Laminate 18 m²: 18 × €6.50 = from €117. The price is determined by the area, the condition of the walls, floors and ceilings, the type of renovation and the chosen finish. An exact estimate can be made after seeing the apartment.

Frequently asked questions

How many estimates should you compare?

More important than the number is that all the estimates are drawn up against the same list of work. Only then can you compare the price per m² and what is included.

Is the cheapest estimate the best choice?

Not always. Check whether it has all the work: demolition, levelling, waterproofing, skimming. If one is missing, it will appear later as extra work.

Does a renovation company have to be in the Register of Construction Merchants?

A company that provides construction (NACE F) services must register; you can check the register in the BIS free of charge. More: Register of Construction Merchants.

What should you do if defects appear after the renovation?

A consumer can submit a claim for non-conformity with the contract within two years of receiving the service, and no later than two months after discovering it. First, ask for the defect to be put right.
