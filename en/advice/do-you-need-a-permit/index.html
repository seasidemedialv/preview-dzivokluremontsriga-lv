Does renovation need a permit

Whether a renovation needs a permit is decided by what the work involves, not by the word “renovation”. Painting, wallpapering and replacing floor covering need no construction documents. Demolishing a non-load-bearing wall requires a construction notice, an opening in a load-bearing wall requires an explanatory memorandum, and in other cases a building design and a building permit are needed.

Prepared by Dzīvokļu Remonts Rīga, updated 12 November 2026. Sources: 10

The regulations do not have a separate type of construction called “replanning of a room group”. The type is chosen by what exactly will be done, and the designer can recommend the most suitable one.

Sources: Cabinet Regulation No. 529 of 02.09.2014 (“Ēku būvnoteikumi”, Building Construction Regulations), amendments up to Cabinet Regulation No. 564 of 22.09.2026; explanation by a sworn advocate of the Latvian Bar on LV portāls, 13.09.2024. Checked 11.11.2026.

When no documents are needed

The Building Construction Regulations exempt from construction-intent documents an interior design project that does not include renovation, reconstruction, restoration or demolition work. Municipalities explain it simply: if only interior work is planned in an apartment, such as painting walls and ceilings, hanging wallpaper or replacing floor covering, construction documentation is not required.

If you are moving walls, openings or utility lines, check the table above again. For more on types of renovation, see cosmetic renovation.

Construction notice, explanatory memorandum and building design

  1. Construction notice For simplified renovation of an apartment in a building of the second or third group, it is filed in BIS before work starts. Attached to it are a plan of the room group showing the changes and a room schedule with room heights, prepared by a registered construction company or construction specialist and approved in BIS. A new apartment cannot be created with a notice.
  2. Explanatory memorandum For an opening in a load-bearing wall, the structural solution of the opening's joints and an assessment of the effect on the strength and stability of the building are attached. Work may start only after the building board notes that the conditions for starting construction work have been met.
  3. Building design and building permit If the work does not fit any of the simpler cases, a building design in minimum composition is prepared and a building permit is obtained.

A certification card has not been used for new work since 01.03.2022. It is used only to complete work for which the card was accepted by 28.02.2022.

Sources: Cabinet Regulation No. 529, paragraphs 7.¹, 7.², 7.³, 7.⁵, 22, 24.4, 108 and 232; Construction Law (Būvniecības likums), Section 17(1). Checked 11.11.2026.

Do you need to agree the renovation with neighbours

An owner may carry out construction work in their own apartment without the agreement of other owners, if the regulations are followed and the work does not affect common property or other apartments. If the work affects common property, such as load-bearing walls, risers or radiators that depend on the building's heating, the consent of the apartment owners' association is needed. If it affects another apartment, the consent of its owner is needed.

  • Opening in a load-bearing wall: Riga municipality explains that, if the building is divided into apartment properties, the consent of more than half (50% + 1) of the apartment owners is needed.
  • Non-load-bearing wall between two apartments: the owners of both apartments agree, and the consent of the others is not needed.
  • Consent is drawn up as a separate agreement, for example a general meeting decision or a voting record.

In an apartment building, a bathroom is placed above similar rooms, but above a neighbour's living rooms it is permitted if the requirements for soundproofing, waterproofing, thermal insulation, fire safety, ventilation and safety are met.

Sources: Law on Residential Properties (Dzīvokļa īpašuma likums), Sections 11 and 16; Riga municipality's explanation on LV portāls, 04.12.2025; Cabinet Regulation No. 529, paragraph 9; LBN 200-21, paragraph 136. Checked 11.11.2026.

Where to submit documents in Riga

Construction is initiated by submitting documents to the building board in the Construction Information System (BIS). Since 01.09.2021 in Riga the functions of the building board are carried out by the City Development Department (Pilsētas attīstības departaments) of Riga municipality (formerly the Riga City Building Board). Notices there are checked by the Architecture Department, with stricter checks since December 2025, and non-compliant notices are annulled.

In the Riga region, documents are likewise submitted in BIS, to the building board of the municipality where the building is. If the building is a cultural monument, the solution is also agreed with the heritage authority.

Sources: Construction Law, Section 14(1); LSM 14.01.2021; Riga municipality on LV portāls, 04.02.2026 and 04.12.2025. Checked 11.11.2026.

What happens if there are no documents

For construction work without an explanatory memorandum or building permit where they are needed, the Construction Law provides for an individual a warning or a fine of up to 400 fine units (up to €2,000). The building board may decide to restore the previous condition at the owner's expense. More: replanning an apartment without approval.

Source: Construction Law, Sections 18 and 25. Checked 11.11.2026.

How much the documents and work cost

Prices are for labour. Materials are calculated separately in the estimate. For replanning documents and approval the price is by estimate: it depends on whether the work affects load-bearing structures, which documents are needed (a construction notice or an explanatory memorandum) and whether a structural engineer's opinion is needed. For the work itself: demolition of walls, floors and finishes from €6/m², plasterboard partition wall from €12/m².

If your work needs approval, write that in the request. For replanning work, see approval of replanning.

Frequently asked questions

Does a bathroom renovation need a permit?

If you are only replacing tiles on the same surfaces and the finish, it is interior work. Whether replacing plumbing and pipes needs a notice, find out from the building board before the work (in Riga the City Development Department). If you combine the bathroom with the toilet by demolishing a non-load-bearing wall, a construction notice is needed. The riser is common property: work on it needs the association's consent and is organised by the building manager.

Can I file the construction notice myself?

The notice is filed in BIS, but the plan of the room group with the changes attached to it is prepared and approved in BIS by a registered construction company or construction specialist.

Is a certification card still needed?

Not for new work. It has not been used since 01.03.2022; a construction notice or an explanatory memorandum is used now.

Can a gas hob be moved during renovation?

Within one room, the user, in agreement with the gas supplier, may replace or move a gas hob and other appliances whose combustion products are not discharged into a flue. An appliance with a flue, for example a gas boiler, needs the supplier's permission even in the same room, a certificate on the condition of the flues and ventilation ducts, and a technical solution. Moving to another room needs the supplier's technical conditions, a flue and ventilation certificate and a building design. The technical solution for moving gas pipes is prepared or accepted by a certified person. Gas work is not part of the renovation estimate.
