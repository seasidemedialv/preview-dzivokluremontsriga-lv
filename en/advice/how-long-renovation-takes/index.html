How long an apartment renovation takes

How long an apartment renovation takes depends on the scope of work and the drying time of the materials, so there is no single figure for every apartment. For a cosmetic renovation the floor area and the condition of the walls matter most; for a full renovation, so do screed drying and documents. Below is what you can plan for: which stages wait for documents and how long the materials dry according to the manufacturers' technical sheets.

What determines the length of a renovation

  • Scope of work: how many rooms, how many m² of walls, whether the wiring and pipes are replaced, whether a new screed is laid.
  • Type of renovation: in a cosmetic renovation the existing utilities stay, in a full renovation the rooms are stripped back to the structure.
  • Documents: if an explanatory memorandum is needed, the work may only start after the building board's mark that the conditions for starting construction work have been met.
  • Drying of materials: screed, self-levelling compound, waterproofing, tile adhesive, grout and paint are not ready for the next job straight away.
  • Decisions along the way: if the tiles, floor covering or doors are not chosen in good time, the work stands still.
  • Building systems: replacing a riser or a radiator depends on when the building manager can shut off the water or heating.

Source: Construction Law, Section 17(1); Cabinet Regulation No. 529, paragraph 108. Checked 11.11.2026.

Material drying times according to manufacturers' sheets

These times come from the technical sheets of specific products and apply to the conditions the manufacturer states. For other products they may differ, and in a cold or damp room materials dry more slowly.

Material What the manufacturer states What you wait for
Ceresit CT 17 primer dries in about 2 hours the next coat
Ceresit CN 76 self-levelling compound (4–50 mm) walkable after 3 hours, tiling after 3 days; a layer up to 1.5 cm dries at 4 mm per day, a thicker one at 3 mm per day (+23 °C, 50% humidity) floor covering
New concrete screed (Quick-Step instructions) at least a week per centimetre up to 4 cm, twice as long if thicker laminate
Cement screed and plaster under tiles (Ceresit CM 17) at least 28 days old, moisture below 4% tiling
Ceresit CL 51 waterproofing second coat after about 1 hour, tiling about 2 hours after the second tiling
Ceresit CM 17 tile adhesive grout walls after at least 12 hours, the floor after at least 24 hours grouting
Ceresit CE 40 grout walkable after 6 hours, water load after 24 hours, full strength after 5 days using the shower
Tikkurila Euro Smart 2 paint second coat after 2 hours (+20 °C, 65% humidity), final hardness in about a month furniture against the walls

The longest wait is usually for a new cement screed. If a 4 cm screed is laid and laminate goes on top, the Quick-Step instructions require at least four weeks. With a self-levelling compound in a thin layer the wait is shorter, so the choice of floor solution affects the whole schedule. More: how long screed, skim coat and tile adhesive take to dry.

Sources: technical sheets for Ceresit CT 17, CN 76, CM 17, CL 51 and CE 40 (Henkel); Quick-Step laminate installation instructions LV; Tikkurila Euro Smart 2 data sheet. Checked 11.11.2026.

How long a bathroom renovation takes

In a bathroom the stages follow one after another, and each has its own waiting time. So a bathroom is usually planned as a separate chain even when work in the rest of the apartment goes on in parallel.

  1. Demolition Old tiles, bath, toilet. If a damaged base or pipes are discovered, the plan changes.
  2. Pipes and base New pipes to the riser, floor levelling or screed. According to the CM 17 sheet, a cement screed under tiles must be at least 28 days old.
  3. Waterproofing The manufacturer of Ceresit CL 51 recommends applying it in at least two coats so that the dry layer is 0.5 mm; waterproofing tape is worked into the joints.
  4. Tiling and grout joints The manufacturer of Ceresit CM 17 allows grouting the floor after at least 24 hours. CE 40 grout withstands water load after 24 hours and reaches full strength after 5 days.
  5. Plumbing Installing the toilet, sink, bath or shower. In corners and next to sanitary fittings the joints are filled with silicone, not grout.

When the shower can be used is determined by the grout and silicone manufacturers' instructions, not by the job list. More on this room: bathroom renovation.

Sources: technical sheets for Ceresit CL 51, CM 17 and CE 40. Checked 11.11.2026.

The deadline in the contract

If no deadline has been agreed, the Consumer Rights Protection Law provides that the service is provided within a reasonable time. If the provider is late, the consumer gives an additional deadline and after it can withdraw from the contract; the money paid must be refunded within 14 days. So it is better to write the end date and the stages into the contract. How to do that: renovation contract.

Source: Consumer Rights Protection Law, Section 30(4), (5) and (7). Checked 11.11.2026.

Frequently asked questions

Why can't the length of a renovation be given before a viewing?

The duration depends on the scope of work and the drying time of the materials. The condition of the walls, of the wiring and pipes, and the floor solution become clear only on site. An exact estimate can be made after seeing the apartment.

Can a new screed be tiled after just a few days?

A cement screed cannot: the Ceresit CM 17 technical sheet requires it to be at least 28 days old with moisture below 4%. The manufacturer of Ceresit CN 76 self-levelling compound allows tiling after 3 days. These are different materials with different requirements.

Does the renovation take longer if a notice or an explanatory memorandum is needed?

Yes, because work that needs an explanatory memorandum may only start after the building board's mark. Plan the preparation and submission of the documents before demolition. Whether documents are needed: does a renovation need a permit.

What happens if the renovation is late?

If the deadline is in the contract, refer to it. The Consumer Rights Protection Law allows you to give an additional deadline and after it to withdraw from the contract; the money must be refunded within 14 days.
