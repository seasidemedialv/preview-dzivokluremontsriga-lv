Riga: from panel buildings to wooden houses

In Riga's neighbourhoods the buildings differ greatly. Purvciems and Pļavnieki have many buildings of the 602, 119 and 467 series, Ķengarags the 103 and 104 series, the Centre pre-war masonry tenement buildings, and Āgenskalns wooden apartment houses and pre-war masonry buildings. This determines what work is usually needed and what to check before renovating. Overview of Riga: apartment renovation in Riga, for example in Purvciems, Ķengarags and Āgenskalns.

The Riga region: private houses, new builds and apartment buildings

Mārupe has private houses, terraced houses and new-build projects, Jūrmala has historic wooden housing, summer houses, private houses and apartment buildings in Kauguri and Dubulti, and Salaspils has apartment-building districts. Work in private houses is in the section private house interiors and renovation.

Where to submit construction documents

If a renovation needs a construction notice or an explanatory memorandum, it is submitted to the building board in the Construction Information System (BIS). In Riga, the functions of the building board have been performed since 01.09.2021 by the City Development Department (Pilsētas attīstības departaments) of the Riga municipality (formerly the Riga City Building Board); in the Riga region by the building board of the relevant district, for example the Mārupe district building board or the Ķekava district building board. When documents are needed: does a renovation need a permit.

Sources: Construction Law, Section 14(1); LSM, 14.01.2021, on the transfer of the functions of the Riga City Building Board. Checked 11.11.2026.
