Detached houses with allotments: what changes in them

In Katlakalns, the area between Bukaišu iela and the Daugava is occupied by low-rise detached houses with allotments. The neighbourhood is in the south of Riga, by the Daugava and the Ķekava border. The rest of the development consists mainly of industrial and public buildings between Bauskas iela and Bukaišu iela and along Lejupes iela, and part of the Katlakalns land was included in Riga's administrative territory only in 1968.

A detached house has no apartment owners' association as an apartment building does, but the questions of construction documents remain. But you yourself need to know what is under the floor and in the walls: how the floor is built, where the water and sewerage run, how old the wires are. This determines whether the renovation will be cosmetic or full. A cosmetic renovation renews the walls, ceilings, floor covering and doors, while the existing wires and pipes stay; a full renovation strips back to the structure and fits new installations, screed and all finishing.

In Riga, for buildings older than 50 years that are not state-protected, the City Development Department can determine the cultural and historical value and the permitted action when it examines a construction proposal. If your house is old and the renovation changes its volume or facade, you have to take this into account. Katlakalns also has a few architectural monuments of local significance, and most of them are along Bauskas iela. If the building is a monument of local significance, the solution is agreed with the cultural heritage unit of the City Development Department. For interior work, such as painting walls, hanging wallpaper or replacing the floor covering, no construction documents are needed.

Bauskas iela, the Southern Bridge and the description in the request

Katlakalns is reached along Bauskas iela and Ziepniekkalna iela, and from the right bank of the Daugava along the Southern Bridge, built from 2004 to 2008, which links Ķengarags with Katlakalns at the junction of Bukaišu iela and Jāņa Čakstes gatve. Houses by the allotments can have a narrow access road, so write whether a vehicle can reach the house and where materials can be placed. A new bathroom in a private house

If a bathroom is fitted from scratch in the house or moved to another room, the order of work is as follows: pipes and the sewerage outlet, the position of the floor drain and the floor slope, waterproofing, tiles, plumbing. For waterproofing, the manufacturer Ceresit CL 51 recommends at least two coats so that the dry layer is 0.5 mm, and waterproofing tape in the joints between wall and floor; collars are used at pipes and the drain.

For the tile adhesive CM 17 the manufacturer requires it to cover at least 65% of the underside of the tile, and in corners, at the floor and at plumbing the joints are filled with silicone, not grout. The bathroom and toilet are ventilated through a natural exhaust duct, so ventilation must also be provided for a new bathroom. In the request, state the dimensions of the bathroom, whether you want a bath or a shower and whether you want underfloor heating. See bathroom renovation in Katlakalns and shower with a floor drain.

Floors in the house: boards, screed and levelling

In a private house the floor can be on wooden beams or on a concrete slab, and this determines the work. If there are sound wooden boards under the old covering, they can be restored. If the floor is concrete and uneven, it is levelled with a self-levelling compound; Ceresit CN 76 is poured in a layer 4–50 mm thick. For laminate, the manufacturer Quick-Step requires unevenness greater than 2 mm per 1 m to be levelled out, and under laminate the moisture of the cement base must be below 2.5%.

In a large room, expansion joints are left in the levelling layer: the manufacturer states an area of up to 36 m², and joints also at room thresholds. See floor levelling in Katlakalns, restoring a wooden floor and uneven floor.

Attic or mansard for living

In a low-rise house you can gain an extra room under the roof. If a mansard is built in the attic, it can be insulated, plasterboard walls and ceilings can be built, wiring can be run and a floor laid. According to Knauf product sheets, the frame of a plasterboard partition wall is made of CW profiles (studs) and UW profiles in the floor and ceiling. If the shape of the roof or the volume of the building changes, it is reconstruction and construction intent documents are needed; approval of reconstruction in Katlakalns is handled by the City Development Department of Riga Municipality (formerly the Riga City Building Board).

If the installation in the mansard is built from scratch, from 01.07.2025 it must be fitted with residual current protection. See mansard renovation in Katlakalns, summer house renovation and private house renovation.

Frequently asked questions

Can I move the bathroom to another room in a private house in Katlakalns without a permit?

Before moving a bathroom to another room, find out from the City Development Department of Riga Municipality whether a construction notice is needed; for simplified renovation (without changing load-bearing structures, volume and use) it is submitted in BIS before work starts. If an opening has to be cut in a load-bearing wall, an explanatory memorandum is needed; in other cases a building design and a building permit are needed. Write your plan in the request.

How long do I have to wait after floor levelling before laying laminate?

The manufacturer of Ceresit CN 76 states that at +23 °C and 50% humidity it dries about 4 mm per day up to a layer of 1.5 cm. Quick-Step states that under laminate the moisture of the cement base must be below 2.5% (CM); the number of drying days alone does not show this.
