Apartment renovation in Ķīpsala - for two-storey wooden rental houses and new projects

Apartment renovation in Ķīpsala is one of two very different jobs. In the historic two-storey wooden rental houses and private houses, floors, walls and wiring are renewed, with care over everything that touches the facade, while in the new apartment projects on the island the interior finishing is completed or the layout is changed.

From fishermen's log cabins to wooden rental houses and new builds

A Riga neighbourhood portal writes that in Ķīpsala the fishermen's small log cabins were over time replaced by small two-storey wooden rental houses and private houses. Today, on the island opposite the city centre, alongside the historic wooden houses there are new apartment projects, as well as university and exhibition complexes. In apartment renovation these are two separate stories.

In a two-storey wooden house, an apartment may occupy one floor, and then there is a neighbour below or above, with whom the wooden floor structure is shared. The condition of the floors, wall finishes and wiring differs in every house, so the list of work depends on the particular apartment. In a new project the apartment may be handed over with a grey or white finish, and then the work is to complete what the developer left.

“Ķīpsalas vēsturiskā apbūve” (the historic development of Ķīpsala) was included in the list of state-protected cultural monuments by a 1998 order of the Ministry of Culture as an urban planning monument of national significance, and in the same list the residential building from 1838 at Balasta dambis 52 is an architectural monument of national significance. In the historic part (Loču iela, Enkura iela, Zvejnieku iela, Ķīpsalas iela, Āzenes iela, Balasta dambis) it is prohibited to replace the original windows and external doors, except where they practically cannot be preserved or are not visible from the public outdoor space (Cabinet Regulation No. 127, paragraph 8.4.1).

If the building is a cultural monument, the solution is agreed with the National Heritage Board. So in a wooden house, start the renovation plan with what will happen to the windows and doors, and plan the interior work around them.

A wooden rental house: the floor and the floor structure

In a wooden house the floorboards may have survived under later coverings. If they are sound, they can be restored, sanded and varnished rather than covered with a new covering. If the boards move or are damaged, the floor structure must be assessed first. For tiles, for example in a bathroom, the manufacturer Ceresit requires a dry, load-bearing, stable and clean base, so on a wooden floor structure you first assess whether it is stable enough.

The partition walls and the floor and wall finishes in an apartment are your property, but the external walls, the roof, the windows and the floor structures with their sound and thermal insulation are common property. So replacing or strengthening a floor structure is not a decision for one apartment. If the apartment still has a stove, write whether you will keep it, because it affects the floor and wall work around it. A new apartment on the island: from the finish to the layout

The new apartments in Ķīpsala are in very different buildings. The former Celms and Bēms gypsum factory was converted into a residential complex in 2003, the first example in Latvia where a factory became an apartment building, while for Riverstone Residence at Ķīpsalas iela 49 six buildings no higher than three floors are planned, and the first three-storey building with 10 apartments was built from 2015 to 2017. So in the request write the name of the project and what was handed over: whether the walls are plastered, whether there is a screed, wiring run to the points and the bathroom and toilet. In a bathroom, waterproofing is applied before the tiles; the manufacturer Ceresit CL 51 recommends at least two coats.

If you want to combine the kitchen with the living room or move the combined bathroom, decide this before the wiring and pipes, because moving it after the finishing costs more. When completing the apartment, an autonomous smoke detector must also be provided, as required by the Fire Safety Regulations currently in force. Approval of reconstruction in Ķīpsala

Approval of reconstruction in Ķīpsala is handled by the City Development Department of the Riga municipality (formerly the Riga City Building Board). For an interior design project that does not include renovation, reconstruction, restoration or demolition work, construction proposal documents are not needed. For demolishing a non-load-bearing wall, according to the Riga municipality's explanation, an architect prepares a plan and a construction notice is submitted in the BIS; the notice is not used if the work results in a new apartment.

In Riga, the department's Architecture Division checks BIS notices, with stricter checks since December 2025, and cancels non-compliant notices, so the plan must match what is actually built. With an explanatory memorandum, work may begin only after the building board's note that the conditions for starting construction have been met. If approval is needed, write that in the request. See replanning approval and replanning.

Frequently asked questions

Can the windows be replaced in a wooden house in Ķīpsala?

In the historic part (Loču iela, Enkura iela, Zvejnieku iela, Ķīpsalas iela, Āzenes iela, Balasta dambis) it is prohibited to replace the original windows and external doors, except where they practically cannot be preserved or are not visible from the public outdoor space (Cabinet Regulation No. 127, paragraph 8.4.1). If the windows have already been replaced with ones that do not match the style, after approval they may be replaced with ones that do. Windows are part of the building's external structures, so the work is also agreed with the building manager.

Can two apartments be combined in a new project in Ķīpsala?

A construction notice is not enough for combining two apartments if the result is a new apartment (Cabinet Regulation No. 529, paragraph 7.⁵); find out the type of document from the City Development Department or from a designer. If the wall between the apartments is not load-bearing, the consent of both owners is needed; if it is load-bearing, also a structural engineer, an explanatory memorandum and the consent of more than half of the apartment owners. After the work, the cadastral survey file is updated.

Where to start with the renovation of an apartment in a wooden house?

With the condition of the floor, the floor structure and the wiring, because the finishing comes last. In the request, describe the condition of the floor and the electrical distribution board; also read where to start a renovation.
