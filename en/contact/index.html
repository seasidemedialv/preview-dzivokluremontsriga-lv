Request

After you submit the form, we contact you within 5 minutes

By sending this, you agree that we use your data to process your request. Privacy policy

What to write in the request

  • Address: street, building number, city.
  • Area of the apartment or rooms in m² and the number of rooms.
  • Building series or year of construction, if you know it.
  • Which rooms to renovate and what work you want: cosmetic or full renovation, bathroom, floors, walls.
  • Whether you will choose the materials yourself.
  • When the renovation is needed: as soon as possible, this month, in 1–3 months or just a price for now.

For businesses: state the type of premises (office, café, stairwell) and the area in m².

What to describe in the “Your situation, briefly” field

In the “Your situation, briefly” field, describe the condition of each room to be renovated, damage to the walls and floors and, for a bathroom, also the plumbing and the position of the risers. You can send photos or the apartment plan to the specialist when they contact you; it is better if no people or documents are visible in them.

If you need replanning

If you plan to demolish a wall, make an opening or combine rooms, write that in the request. Such work usually needs documents: approval of apartment reconstruction.

Frequently asked questions

Why is there no phone number?

We accept requests only in the form, because the price of a renovation depends on the address, area, work and other details, and in the form you can write them in one place. After you submit the form, we contact you within 5 minutes.

Can I write the request in Russian or English?

Yes. The form is in Latvian, English and Russian, and you can write your request in any of them.

What if I do not know the building series?

Write the address and, if you know it, the year the building was constructed. The series can also be determined later.

Send the address, the floor area and a short description of the work. After you submit the form, we contact you within 5 minutes, confirm the work and agree the price before the job.
