The external structures of a loggia are common property of the building, so glazing and joining a loggia to a room have their own rules. Write the loggia dimensions, the building series and the condition; the price is by estimate.

Loggia glazing in Riga

The Building Construction Regulations allow a loggia to be glazed without construction documents if a facade glazing sketch or passport has been agreed with the building board, or if the work conforms to a model loggia glazing sketch approved by the municipality (Cabinet Regulation No. 529, sub-paragraph 7.4.7). The annex to Riga's Territory Use and Building Regulations contains standard loggia glazing sketches for buildings of the 103, 104, 119, 464, 467 and 602 series.

If your building has an approved sketch or passport, ask the building manager about it before ordering the glazing. The loggia finishing is done after the glazing has been installed.

Cabinet Regulation No. 529 of 02.09.2014 (“Ēku būvnoteikumi”, Building Construction Regulations), sub-paragraph 7.4.7; LV portāls, e-consultation on loggia glazing in Riga, 2023. Checked 11.11.2026.

Can a loggia be joined to a room

The external structures of balconies and loggias are common property (Law on Residential Properties, Section 4). Joining a loggia to a room affects common property, so the consent of the apartment owners' association is needed. Find out from the building board which documents are needed in your particular building; in Riga that is the City Development Department. If the work affects load-bearing walls, see apartment replanning.

Heat on the loggia: radiator and underfloor heating

A radiator in an apartment is common property if its operation depends on the building's heating. So moving a radiator to the loggia or connecting water underfloor heating to the building's heating affects common property and needs the association's consent; the work is organised by the building manager.

Electric underfloor heating is not connected with the building's heating. If the electrical wiring is reconstructed for it, residual current protection is needed, and the connection is made by a qualified electrician. Underfloor heating installation is from €16/m². More: underfloor heating installation and internal wall insulation.

Loggia finishing: order of work

  1. Glazing If the loggia is glazed, this is done before the finishing; find out the condition of the structures from the building manager.
  2. Wiring Lighting points, sockets and, if one is laid, the connection of electric underfloor heating.
  3. Floor Levelling the base and, if needed, underfloor heating. For Ceresit CN 76 self-levelling compound, the manufacturer states that it can be walked on after 3 hours and tiled after 3 days.
  4. Walls and ceiling Plasterboard or painted ceiling, wall skimming and painting, or cladding.
  5. Covering and final work Laminate, vinyl flooring or tiles, window sills and skirting boards.

What this work includes

  • floor levelling and laying the covering;
  • laying electric underfloor heating;
  • building the ceiling and painting the walls;
  • installing sockets and lighting points.

What is calculated separately

Frequently asked questions

Does loggia glazing need a permit?

Documents are not needed if a glazing sketch or passport for the building has been agreed with the building board or the work conforms to the municipality's model sketch. In Riga there are standard sketches for the 103, 104, 119, 464, 467 and 602 series; ask the building manager about your own building.

Can water underfloor heating from the building's heating be installed on a loggia?

The heating elements of the building's heating are common property, so such a connection needs the association's consent, and the work is organised by the building manager. Electric underfloor heating is not connected with the building's heating.

How much does loggia renovation cost?

The total price is by estimate. For example, floor levelling, underfloor heating and laminate on a 4 m² loggia together are from €116 for the work. An exact estimate can be made after seeing the apartment.
