What a “Khrushchyovka” is

“Khrushchyovkas” is the name for buildings built in the second half of the 1950s, for example buildings of the 1-317 and similar projects. They are mostly two- to five-storey buildings, and five-storey ones have no lift. The oldest were built of sand-lime brick, the later ones of panel blocks. The apartments are small: a one-room apartment about 29–35 m², a two-room 39–41 m², a three-room about 51 m².

In the oldest five-storey buildings the bathroom and toilet are often combined, and the size depends on the particular apartment. Check in your apartment and in the request write the length and width of the room.

What to choose in a small combined bathroom

In a Khrushchyovka bathroom the main question is space. A bath takes up a whole wall, while a shower with a floor drain frees space for a washing machine. If you need a bath, think about a shorter model or a sink above the washing machine. Each decision changes where the pipes go, so it is decided before demolition.

  • Bath or shower with a floor drain: a drain needs a sloping floor, and the floor level by the door may change.
  • A toilet with a concealed cistern in the front wall, or an ordinary floor-standing toilet.
  • A place for the washing machine: water supply, drain pipe and socket before the tiling.
  • The direction of the door and the threshold, if the floor level rises.

For more ideas for a small room, see the page small bathroom renovation, and for a combined room with a toilet, the page combined bathroom renovation.

Old pipes, wiring and materials

In many homes the wiring was built in the Soviet era, and the wires are 30–50 years old. The electrical installation is inspected every 10 years, and if the installation is changed in the bathroom, the new one needs residual current protection. Water pipes from the riser to the appliances are part of the apartment, and they can be replaced before the tiling.

In buildings built before the asbestos ban, some materials may contain asbestos, for example pipe insulation. It cannot be reliably identified by appearance; it is determined in a laboratory. Demolition of asbestos may be carried out only with the permission of the State Labour Inspectorate. If in doubt, order an analysis before demolition; more on the page asbestos in an apartment.

The Fire Safety Regulations currently in force (Cabinet Regulation No. 238), paragraphs 56 and 57; LBN 261-15 amendments from 01.07.2025; Cabinet Regulation No. 852, paragraph 25; European Commission SAMANCTA on asbestos. Checked 11.11.2026.

Neighbours and sound

Cityreal.lv writes that Khrushchyovkas have weak sound insulation, so demolition and drilling will be audible to the neighbours. The Riga Municipal Police explain that everyday noise is usually not an offence, but disturbing a person's peace can be an administrative offence, and the apartment owners' association can set house rules. Ask the building manager whether the building has set times for renovation, and warn the neighbours.

Administrative Penalties Law (on offences in the fields of administration, public order and the use of the official language), Section 11; Law on Residential Properties (Dzīvokļa īpašuma likums), Section 10(1) points 6 and 9. Checked 11.11.2026.

The work in the usual order

  1. Plan and choices Bath or shower, the place for the washing machine, the toilet.
  2. Demolition Old tiles, paint and appliances are removed; if asbestos is suspected, analysis first.
  3. Pipes and wiring The pipes up to the riser and the electrical points are replaced.
  4. Levelling and waterproofing Old walls are levelled, a slope is formed in the floor if there will be a drain, and waterproofing is applied.
  5. Tiles and appliances Tiling, grouting with silicone in the corners, and installing the plumbing.

Separate jobs: demolition of old tiles from €9/m², waterproofing from €6/m², tiling from €24/m², floor drain installation from €100 per drain, installing a toilet during a renovation from €50 per toilet.

Frequently asked questions

Can the toilet be separated from the bathroom in a Khrushchyovka?

It depends on the size of the room and where the risers and sewage run. Building a new partition is replanning, so check whether a construction notice is needed. In the request write the dimensions of the room and where the risers run.

Can the old tiles be left and new ones fixed on top?

Left under new tiles, the old finish hides the condition of the walls and any missing waterproofing. If the tiles are old, they are removed so that waterproofing can be laid under the new ones.

Can a shower without a tray be fitted in a Khrushchyovka bathroom?

Yes, but the floor needs a slope towards the drain, and that can raise the floor. More on the page shower with a floor drain.

How do I request a renovation in a Khrushchyovka?

Write the address, the floor, the dimensions of the room, whether the bath and toilet are in one room, and where the risers run. After you submit the form, we contact you within 5 minutes.
