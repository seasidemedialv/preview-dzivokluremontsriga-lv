Kitchen renovation in Riga

Kitchen renovation in Riga usually means finishing the walls and ceiling, a new floor covering, tiles above the worktop and electrical points for the new furniture and appliances.

A kitchen has water, sewage, electricity, ventilation and sometimes gas in one place, so the job list is drawn up from the furniture plan. In the request, write the kitchen area and the building series or year.

Small kitchen renovation in a series building

In series buildings the kitchen is small. According to a compilation of standard plans, in the 119 series the kitchen is 6–7 m², in the 602 series about 7–8 m², and in the 104 series 8–9 m². In such a room almost the whole wall is taken up by cabinets, so the positions of sockets, water and drain outlets are set by the new furniture drawing, not by the old layout.

The kitchen's fittings, finishing and utilities up to the risers are part of the apartment (Law on Residential Properties, Section 3), and they can be changed without the agreement of other owners if the work does not affect common property. The riser itself is common property. If it has to be replaced or shut off, start with the building manager: riser replacement in an apartment.

Ventilation and the cooker hood

In residential buildings the kitchen is ventilated through a natural extract duct (LBN 231-15). If the building has a shared ventilation system, the kitchen's air duct may only be connected to its own duct.

The Fire Safety Regulations currently in force prohibit connecting a mechanical ventilation device to a natural ventilation duct in an apartment building if the room has a gas hob or other gas appliance and there is no constant air exchange. A cooker hood with a fan can be such a device, so in a kitchen with gas, discuss connecting the hood to the duct with the building manager. Ventilation ducts in rooms with gas appliances are cleaned and inspected at least once every five years.

The gas hob during renovation

Gas work is not part of the renovation estimate. With the agreement of the gas supplier, the user may replace or move a gas hob within one room (LBN 241-15, paragraph 7). Moving the hob to another room needs the supplier's technical conditions, a report on the flue and ventilation ducts, and a building design. If the hob stays in place, write that in the request so that the backsplash and furniture are planned around it.

The order of work in a kitchen renovation

  1. Furniture and appliance plan First the furniture, hob, oven, fridge and dishwasher are chosen, because the positions of sockets and water outlets depend on them.
  2. Demolition The old furniture, tiles and floor covering are removed.
  3. Electricity and pipes Cables and pipes are run to the new positions. If the electrical installation is rebuilt, it must be fitted with residual current protection.
  4. Walls and ceiling Plastering or skimming, priming and painting. The manufacturer Tikkurila states that skimmed and absorbent surfaces are primed before painting.
  5. Floor The base is levelled and tiles, vinyl flooring or laminate are laid. Quick-Step states that before laminate, unevenness over 2 mm per 1 m is levelled out.
  6. Backsplash The tiles above the worktop are laid when the exact height of the worktop is known. In corners and next to the worktop the joints are filled with silicone, not grout (Ceresit CM 17).
  7. Furniture and connection The furniture, sink and appliances are installed, and the sockets and light points are connected.

What to decide before the renovation

  • A furniture drawing with the positions and dimensions of all the equipment.
  • The floor covering: tiles, vinyl flooring or laminate. A comparison: tiles or laminate in the kitchen.
  • The backsplash material and height.
  • Whether the kitchen is combined with a room: then see combining a kitchen with a room.
  • Whether you will choose the materials yourself. Write that in the request.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a small kitchen renovation cost?

In a small kitchen the price is set by the job list, not only the area. Tiling is from €24/m², painting from €4/m², each electrical point from €12 per point. An exact estimate can be made after seeing the apartment.

Does a kitchen renovation need approval?

The municipality explains that no construction documentation is needed for painting walls and ceilings, hanging wallpaper and replacing a floor covering. If you demolish a wall or move a gas hob to another room, documents are needed. More: does a renovation need a permit.

Can the sink be moved to another wall?

The pipes in the apartment up to the riser are part of the apartment, so you can change the position of the sink in your own kitchen. If the riser has to be touched, the work is agreed with the building manager. The new position has to be known before the wall finishing, so that the pipes are run in time.

Can a kitchen cooker hood be connected to the ventilation duct?

If the kitchen has a gas hob and no constant air exchange, connecting a mechanical device to the natural ventilation duct is prohibited. Without gas, discuss the solution with the building manager, because the duct is part of the building's system.
