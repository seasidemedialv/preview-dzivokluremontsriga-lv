Toilet renovation in an apartment

Toilet renovation in Riga and the Riga region means renewing a separate toilet: demolition of the old finish, tiles for walls and floor, a new toilet and a tidy space around the risers.

The room is small, but sewage, water supply and ventilation all meet in it. Tiling costs from €24/m², and installing a toilet during a renovation from €50 per toilet.

How a toilet differs from a bathroom

Nobody washes in a toilet, so the water load on the floor and walls is lower than in a bathroom. The floor is still washed, though, and if the toilet or its supply starts to leak, the water ends up on the floor. So in a toilet too the floor and the lower part of the walls are tiled, and the joints by the toilet and in the floor corners are filled with silicone, not grout.

In apartment buildings a toilet may also contain risers. Risers are the common property of the apartment owners, so work on them needs the association's consent and they cannot simply be walled in or moved. The owner must allow specialists authorised by the association or the building manager to access the building's utilities, so a hatch is left in the riser box.

Law on Residential Properties (Dzīvokļa īpašuma likums), Section 4(1) point 1 and Section 10(1) point 7; Ceresit CM 17 technical data sheet. Checked 11.11.2026.

Renovating a small toilet: what to decide

  • A floor-standing or wall-hung toilet with a frame: a wall-hung one needs a front wall that takes up depth, but the floor under the toilet is easier to wash.
  • The riser box: plasterboard with a hatch, or a tiled opening panel.
  • A small sink for washing hands: it needs a water supply and a drain pipe before the tiling.
  • A shelf or small cabinet above the toilet or in the box.
  • Light and ventilation: whether there will be a lighting point and a fan.

In a small toilet the tile size affects the number of cuts. Around the risers, the toilet supply and the door opening there are many corners, and that affects the amount of work.

Ventilation in the toilet

In residential buildings toilets are ventilated through natural extract ducts. If there is a duct grille in the toilet, it is not tiled over or covered with a box. In a toilet and other rooms without gas appliances the ducts are cleaned and checked at least every 10 years, in rooms with gas appliances at least every five years. Before the renovation, ask the building manager when the duct was last checked.

LBN 231-15, paragraph 90.²; the Fire Safety Regulations currently in force (Cabinet Regulation No. 238), sub-paragraph 80.2. Checked 11.11.2026.

Order of work

  1. Demolishing the toilet and finish The toilet, old tiles or paint are removed. If the risers are old, this is the easiest moment to decide on replacing them.
  2. Pipes If the type or position of the toilet changes, the supply and the sewage connection are rerouted. For a wall-hung toilet a frame is installed.
  3. Box and walls The riser box and front wall are built, and the walls and floor are levelled.
  4. Tiling The floor and walls are tiled and grouted, with silicone in the corners and by the toilet.
  5. Toilet and light The toilet, sink and lighting point are installed.

How much a toilet renovation costs

Prices are for labour. Materials are calculated separately in the estimate. The total for a toilet renovation is made up of separate jobs. Example for a toilet with 14 m² of wall and floor to be tiled:

  • demolition of old tiles 14 m² × €9 = from €126;
  • tiling 14 m² × €24 = from €336;
  • installing a toilet during a renovation from €50 per toilet.

Total from €512 for these three jobs. A plasterboard box, a lighting point from €12 per point and replacing a section of riser from €100 per riser may be added. The price is affected by the condition of the walls, whether the pipes and risers are replaced, and the chosen tiles. All prices are on the prices page.

Frequently asked questions

My cistern is only leaking. Do I need a toilet renovation?

No. If only the cistern mechanism, a seal or a tap needs replacing, that is a plumbing repair, not a toilet renovation. For that kind of job, see santehnikisriga.lv.

Can the risers in a toilet be tiled over?

They can be boxed in, but access must be left at the risers, for example a hatch. The owner must allow the building manager's specialists to carry out work on maintaining the building's utilities.

Can a toilet be combined with a bathroom?

If the wall is not load-bearing, according to Riga municipality's explanation an architect's plan and a construction notice in BIS are needed; an opening in a load-bearing wall needs an explanatory memorandum and, if the building is divided into apartment properties, the consent of more than half of the owners. These documents are not included in the toilet renovation price. More on the page combining a bathroom and toilet.

Can a toilet renovation be done together with the bathroom?

Yes. Write the dimensions of both rooms in the request. After you submit the form, we contact you within 5 minutes.
