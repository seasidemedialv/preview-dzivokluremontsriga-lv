Hallway and corridor renovation in Riga

Hallway and corridor renovation in Riga means a lot of work in a narrow space: a floor walked on in outdoor shoes, several doors to other rooms, walls by the wardrobe and a ceiling that may hold wiring.

The corridor connects the whole apartment, so its renovation is usually planned last or together with the rooms. In the form, write the area and the number of doors.

The floor by the entrance: tiles or laminate

In a hallway the floor takes sand, damp and the load of shoes. Tiles can go by the entrance and laminate or vinyl flooring further along the corridor. A comparison of the two coverings: tiles or laminate in the kitchen and hallway.

If the floor is tiled, the manufacturer Ceresit states for CM 17 tile adhesive that grouting on a floor can be done after at least 24 hours. For CE 40 grout, the floor can be walked on after 6 hours, and it reaches full strength after 5 days. The transition between tiles and laminate is usually made at the door threshold. For Quick-Step laminate, a gap of at least 8–10 mm is recommended also at thresholds and under doors.

Doors, walls and ceilings

From a corridor there are usually doors to every room, so replacing doors is a large part of the work. Installing interior doors is from €100 per door; you choose and buy the doors yourself, separately. If both doors and floor are replaced, the floor is laid first, so that the bottom of the doors can be fitted to the new covering.

In a narrow corridor the walls wear most by the wardrobe and the switches. Plasterboard or suspended ceilings are from €15/m², and wiring for new light fittings can be run under them. More: plasterboard ceilings and interior door installation.

Smoke detector and electrics in the hallway

The Fire Safety Regulations currently in force require an stand-alone smoke detector in an apartment in an apartment building, at least one on each floor of the apartment, installed and maintained according to the manufacturer's requirements. If a new ceiling is being fitted in the corridor, allow for the detector's position now.

The same regulations provide for an electrical installation inspection every 10 years with an inspection report. If the wiring is changed in the hallway, the rebuilt installation needs residual current protection. More: old electrical wiring in an apartment.

Cabinet Regulation No. 238 of 19.04.2016 (Fire Safety Regulations), paragraphs 56, 57, 119 and 120; LBN 261-15 amendments of 17.06.2025. Checked 11.11.2026.

Order of work in the hallway and corridor

  1. Demolition The old doors, skirting boards, floor covering and, if needed, the old finish.
  2. Wiring New light points, switches and sockets while the walls and ceilings are not yet finished.
  3. Ceilings and walls Plasterboard ceilings or skimming, then priming and painting the walls.
  4. Floor Tiles by the entrance, laminate or vinyl flooring in the corridor, the transition at the threshold.
  5. Doors and skirting boards Doors, door casings and skirting boards are installed on the finished floor.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a corridor renovation cost?

A corridor estimate is made by the work. For example, 8 m² of tiled floor and four interior doors are from €592 for labour. An exact estimate can be made after seeing the apartment.

What should be done first: the doors or the floor?

Usually the floor is laid first and then the doors are installed, so that the bottom of the doors can be fitted to the height of the new covering. For laminate, an expansion gap is left at the threshold and under the doors.

Does a hallway need a smoke detector?

The regulations require at least one stand-alone smoke detector on each floor of the apartment. The position is chosen at the ceiling; the State Fire and Rescue Service (VUGD) recommends rooms where people sleep or spend time. If a new ceiling is being fitted in the corridor, allow for the position in good time.
